Route schema

Every sport is served at /betting-tips/<Sport>/<id>, and individual events at /betting-tips/<Sport>/<Competition>/<Group>/<Home>_v_<Away>/<id>?event_id=<n>. Event pages list markets as Money Line, Puck Line, Game Totals, Pointspread, Handicap, Win Match 2-way, Total 180s, Most 180s, Handicap 2-way, Total Legs, Frame Betting, Top Team1 Batsman and Outright Winner. A single event page can carry five or more markets, which is what makes the "investigate every market" requirement tractable from OLBG's own vocabulary.

Two route traps. Ice Hockey is id 13; id 11 is American Football. Gaelic sports are split: Gaelic Football is 25 and Hurling is 26. A layer that uses 11 for hockey, or that assumes one Gaelic route, is wrong.

Defects that change how OLBG can be used

  1. Times are US Eastern. A handball fixture OLBG lists as "Today 14:00" is 20:00 CEST on the league's own page. Six hours, every row.
  2. Prices are blank on some event pages. The NHL event page for Carolina at St. Louis renders "WIN @" with no number after it, while the tip counts render normally.
  3. "No tips found." The golf hub returned that string on a day a PGA TOUR event was in progress, so a listing-driven desk would conclude golf has no fixtures.
  4. Outright-only sports. Rugby League returned three 2027 futures and no match market.
  5. Tip share is not a probability. "15/26 Win Tips 58%" is the share of tipsters, not a chance of winning, and it is not used as a pricing input here.

How this desk uses OLBG

As a cross-check that a fixture is being offered to bettors, and as the source of the event URL printed on rows that have one. Never as a price, never as a probability, and never as a substitute for a governance source.
