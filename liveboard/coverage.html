“Official input” counts rows whose probability came from a governing body rather than from OLBG's tip split. Four sports have at least one such row: Baseball, Basketball (EuroLeague), Snooker and Rugby Union (URC).

Reading the counts

Fixtures are the first page of each OLBG hub, not a complete worldwide card. OLBG's own “Load More Tips” control was not followed, so these counts are slices.

High means our probability reached 70%. A tip-share row also needs at least six tips before it can be graded high, so a 2-of-2 split never becomes a certainty.

High and in band is the intersection the brief asks for. Because −250 is itself a 71.4% price, that intersection is about one and a half percentage points wide. See the method and F-14.

Research aid, not betting advice. Prices here are originated by this project, not quotes from a bookmaker. OLBG supplies the fixture list and its published tip shares; governing bodies supply every other number. Where an official number was not obtained, the value is blank and the row appears on Flagged for review.
