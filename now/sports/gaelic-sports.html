Ballyhale and Tullaroan are level on quality, so the three-point start is the bet

Shamrocks Ballyhale v Tullaroan

Tullaroan +3.5 · Handicap 3.5 · 65.1% · -187 medium

Tullaroan +3.5 is the call in the Kilkenny senior hurling final at UPMC Nowlan Park. These are the two most decorated clubs in the county, Ballyhale with 21 titles and Tullaroan with 20, and it is the first time the top two on the roll of honour have met in the final. Ballyhale beat Thomastown 1-18 to 1-16, a two-point margin, and Tullaroan are back in a decider for the first time since 1994.
