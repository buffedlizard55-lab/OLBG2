FR-01 · Confidence-rule divergence · cross-sport

Five layers used at least three different definitions of a high-confidence pick

The hub graded 25 picks high at >=65% probability inside the band; 22 of those 25 are below 70%. The verified desk used 72% (or a 70% band selection), the board labelled 42 picks high, and the slate marked rows high at a price-only band (one ATP pick at 64.97% was labelled high). This consolidation applies one rule - >=70% AND fair decimal 1.40-101.00 - and produces 15 high picks. Every other pick is medium or low with the reason on the row.

FR-02 · Duplicate fixtures and conflicting prices · cross-sport

755 layer rows collapse to 312 fixtures; 208 fixtures appear in two or more layers

208 of the 312 fixtures were captured by more than one session. The same game can carry different model probabilities because each layer ran its own model. Example: Minnesota Wild at Calgary is 70.84% on the verified row but 67.38% on the hub row; Boston v Nashville is 63.69% on the board row and 66.01% on the hub row. One row per fixture is kept, the layer used is recorded in provenance, and the other layers are not blended into the number.
