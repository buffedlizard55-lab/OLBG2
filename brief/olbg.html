The blocking finding

OLBG does not serve its event tables to a non-browser client. Two sport index pages and one event page returned the literal text "No tips found." on requests made at 19:29Z, 19:30Z and 19:44Z today. The editorial copy arrives, the rows do not. The same behaviour was recorded by the preceding desk session at 17:02Z. Consequences:
  • The desk can reproduce OLBG's taxonomy and market vocabulary, and does.
  • It cannot produce "the full list of matches listed on OLBG" from OLBG itself. The fixture list is therefore taken from each governing body and marked with that body's link.
  • No OLBG price, tip percentage or consensus figure is used as an input to any line here.

What OLBG does show, and what we do with it

  • Sport menu and ids — reproduced above.
  • Event ids — the darts capture carries twelve, including 32328 for Littler v O'Connor and 32338 for the European Championship outright. They are recorded for traceability only.
  • Editorial definitions — money line, puck line, totals, set betting, games handicap. Quoted, not priced from.
  • Tip counts and Value Rating — described by OLBG as tip share versus implied probability. Not readable today without JavaScript, and not used.
