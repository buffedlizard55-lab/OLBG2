Mathematical Line Origination, Pricing Identity & Confidence Rules

All prices in /portal/ are originated independently from official governing-body performance data in scripts/portal_model.py. Zero bookmaker quotes are scraped, read, or compared.

1. Fair No-Vig Pricing & Focus-Band Enforcement (-250 to +10000)

fair_decimal  = round(1.0 / p_model, 2)
fair_american = +round((fair_decimal - 1) * 100)        if fair_decimal >= 2.00
                -round(100 / (fair_decimal - 1))        if fair_decimal <  2.00
in_focus_band = (1.40 <= fair_decimal <= 101.00) and (-250 <= fair_american <= +10000)

Deriving the American quote directly from the rounded fair decimal guarantees zero rounding drift between displayed formats. Whenever a match's moneyline favorite exceeds 71.43% (sub--250 chalk), our engine never promotes that chalk moneyline; instead it evaluates standard .5 half-unit handicaps, set lines, leg lines, or puck lines (never synthetic .25/.75 lines). Every featured high-confidence pick explicitly discloses whether it qualifies under Tier H1 (p_model >= 70.0% inside [-250, -233]) or Tier H2 (multi-factor high conviction inside [-250, +10000], including plus-money outrights).
