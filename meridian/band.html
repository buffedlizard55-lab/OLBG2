The −250 to +10000 board

Where the odds window and the confidence rule actually overlap.

The brief asks for high-confidence picks in the −250 to +10000 window. Those two rules pull in opposite directions, and this page shows exactly where they meet.

37picks inside the band
8of them high confidence
0picks outside the band
71.4%the chance −250 implies

The arithmetic. −250 is a decimal price of 1.40, which implies 71.43%. So a two-way selection cannot be both "more than 70% likely" and "longer than −250" unless it sits between 70.0% and 71.43%. That is why the shortlist is full of alternate lines — puck lines, run lines, set handicaps and totals — rather than short-priced favourites.

What we did instead of fudging it. Every market for every fixture was originated, and the pick for each fixture is the strongest in-band selection. A 78% favourite whose money line sits at −355 is not published as a high-confidence pick; its handicap or total is, if that line is genuinely inside the band.
