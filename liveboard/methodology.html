Method

Two sources of probability, never blended: a governing-body number where one was read, and OLBG's published tip split where one was not. The basis is printed on every row.

1. Two kinds of probability

Official input — a probability computed from a governing body's own number. Four sports have one: Baseball (season records, log5 with a four-point home adjustment), Basketball's three EuroLeague games (points scored minus points conceded per game, a three-point home edge, a twelve-point spread), Snooker (world and one-year ranking money on a log scale at 0.45, so an eight-place gap is a real gap and not a certainty), and Rugby Union's three URC games (a single round of results, shrunk hard towards the mean because one round is a thin sample).

Tip share — the other eleven sports. OLBG publishes the split for the one market it highlights. It is smoothed as (tips + 1) ÷ (total + 2), so a 1-of-1 tip becomes 66.7% rather than 100%, and a tip-share row needs at least six tips before it can be graded high. That gate is why a 2-of-2 split shows as medium here and a 26-of-31 split shows as high.

2. From probability to price

Fair decimal is 1 ÷ p. The offered price is 1 + (fair − 1) × (1 − margin), so the margin is taken on the profit and a favourite is never priced at a shorter decimal than its fair price. American odds follow from the offered decimal.

3. Grading and the band

High is p ≥ 0.70. Medium is 0.60 to 0.70. Low is below that. The band is −250 to +10000 measured on the fair price.

A fair 70% shot is -233; a fair 71.4% shot is -250. Once a 4-6% book margin is taken on the profit, the two published gates - at least 70% and no shorter than -250 - intersect in roughly a 70.0-71.4% window. That arithmetic is fixed by the brief, not chosen here, and it is the reason the count of in-band high-confidence selections is small.

4. What is deliberately absent

  • No bookmaker price. OLBG's event pages render market labels and tip splits with the price fields empty for a logged-out visitor. Nothing was borrowed from elsewhere.
  • No injury claim. No official availability list was obtained, so none is asserted. Every prediction card says so.
  • No converted kick-off times. OLBG mixes US Eastern with UK local; the original string is published untouched.
  • No completed rows. Six rows truncated on the hub were dropped rather than finished by eye.
  • No carried-over numbers. Every figure on this board was read in this session; nothing comes from another desk's capture.
