Automation boundary

CI rebuilds all three static layers, checks local links and tests model invariants. It does not fetch or validate live official schedules. A production collector needs source-specific retrieval, raw evidence capture, timestamps, expiry rules and a fail-closed publication gate. Until that exists, this site cannot honestly claim automatic, up-to-date coverage for every sport and market.
