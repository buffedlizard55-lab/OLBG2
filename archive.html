Archive of earlier layers

Each directory below is a complete, previously published desk. They are kept because deleting evidence is worse than duplicating it — but they overlap, they disagree, and their counts must never be added together. This page exists so the front door can be one page while nothing is lost.

Do not sum these layers. The same match appears in several with different prices. MLB game 849805 is published at 0.533, 0.594, 0.603 and 0.651 by four different layers. They are different snapshots taken with different inputs, not four independent confirmations. Repo review records the detail.

How this desk relates to them

  • The front page, Overview, and every page in the navigation are generated by scripts/unified_build.py from data/unified/ and data/cadence/.
  • Fixture facts, prices and predictions come from the 23 October capture; the OLBG event list is re-parsed from the stored page bodies of the same day.
  • Nothing in an archived layer is edited, and no archived layer is deleted.

What this desk is. A dated, source-linked research record of sport listed on OLBG, of our own originated prices, and of confidence-graded selections. It is not a live odds feed and it does not place bets.

Every price, probability and confidence label is our own estimate, derived from the official statistics named on the row. Values that could not be confirmed are left blank and listed on Flagged for review. Capture times and source files are printed on every table.
