Methodology

How a line is originated, how a pick is made, and exactly which parts are verified facts versus disclosed modelling assumptions.

1. Inventory

OLBG's sport taxonomy is taken from its sitemap ↗, which lists 22 sports with numeric ids. Each in-scope sport's /betting-tips/<Sport>/<id> page is read and every fixture recorded with its event id, competition, displayed time, market, OLBG's top community selection and tip counts. Each row records retrieved_via and as_of so a live-fetched row is never mixed with a dated snapshot row.

2. Evidence tiers

TierInputUsed for

noneNothing verifiable.No line is published; the row is flagged.

3. Probability

A logistic function of the rating gap: p = 1 / (1 + exp(-Δ / S)). Fair no-vig decimal price is 1 / p. Half-point lines use floor(x) + 0.5, the convention that avoids a push.

  • ODI and T20I ICC ratings live on different scales (ODI 0-160, T20I 0-300), so each format gets its own logistic scale.
  • No home-advantage term is applied to cricket because no official, in-session verifiable coefficient was available. Both cricket fixtures modelled are at neutral venues in the UAE, which limits the impact. Flagged as a limitation.
  • Rugby union Tier-2 lines use a logistic on the league-points gap with the draw probability held at the published competition base rate and the remaining mass split between the two sides.

4. Confidence rule

LabelCondition
highModel probability ≥ 0.70 on verified Tier 1/2 inputs with verified form, or a Tier 1 rating gap ≥ 0.55 corroborated by a verified same-series head-to-head win.
mediumModel probability ≥ 0.60 on verified Tier 1/2 inputs, or ≥ 0.70 on Tier 3 base rates.
lowAny other published line, and any line whose divergence from an official published probability exceeds 15 percentage points.
unverifiedNo line could be originated from a verifiable official source.
Official-divergence guard. Where a governing body publishes its own probability for the same fixture, we compute the gap. If it exceeds 15.0 percentage points, confidence is capped at LOW and the disagreement is flagged. We never silently override an official figure with our own.

5. Predictions

A prediction is written only for a high-confidence pick, must be at least 30 words, must name the selection unambiguously, and may only use facts recorded in data/lines.json or data/analyse verified-form blocks. Each fact is published under the prediction with its source link. The word count is asserted at build time.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not de-vig bookmaker prices, because OLBG renders them client-side and they were not retrievable (F-02).
  • We do not use OLBG community tip percentages as a probability input. That would echo the crowd rather than originate a line. Tip counts are shown as separate, clearly labelled community data.
  • We do not fill a blank cell with an estimate. An unverifiable value stays blank and is flagged.
  • We do not name a player, injury, score or date in a prediction unless it was verified.
