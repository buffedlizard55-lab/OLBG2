DL-05needs review

No originated price lands inside the brief's -250 to +10000 window

What was seen. The brief's two conditions - a greater than 70% chance and a price between -250 and +10000 - intersect in a window 1.43 probability points wide (p from 0.7000 to 0.714286). The closest of the 288 originated prices is Under 24.5 games at p = 0.7242, a fair -263, which is 0.99 points above the window on the short side.

How it is handled. With half-point lines and a 4.5-game margin standard deviation, consecutive lines step by about four probability points, so a 1.43-point window is stepped over. The five published picks are the highest-confidence match winners (p from 0.7336 to 0.9216) and every one is labelled 'outside the -250 window' on the picks page rather than reclassified. The alternative - publishing a quarter-game line purely to sit inside the window - would be a fabricated market, not a price.

Source: www.atptour.com
