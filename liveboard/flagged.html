F-13 — The −250 floor and the 70% floor overlap in a narrow window

medium None

A fair 70% probability is about −233 and a fair 75% is about −300 after a five per cent margin. Requiring both 'at least 70%' and '−250 or longer' therefore selects roughly the 70–75% band. Strong selections such as Gloucester at 89% (−543) and Mark Williams at 88% are correctly excluded from the band, and the Top picks page shows them separately so the exclusion is visible rather than silent.

What we did instead: Both conditions are applied exactly as the brief states. The five qualifying single matches are listed in the executive summary; the per-sport top five ignore the band and show the true highest-confidence rows.
