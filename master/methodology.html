Overview / Methodology

Methodology

Short version: official numbers in, our own prices out, one selection per match, and a public list of everything that could not be supported.

1. What is on the desk

Two pools are joined. The OLBG pool is every in-scope tip parsed from the embedded payload of OLBG's own pages, captured 2026-10-23 between 19:28Z and 20:32Z; it supplies 142 matches and their quoted prices. The official pool is every fixture in the NHL, MLB and EuroLeague responses captured in the same window; it supplies 93 fixtures. They are joined on club nickname plus an eight-hour start-time window, never on a guessed id, and 3 rows carry both.

2. Where a price comes from

Originated. For a fixture with official season records we compute a probability and print the fair price that matches it. Ice hockey uses points pace and goal differential with a home-ice term; EuroLeague uses win rate and point differential; MLB uses winning percentage with a home term. Totals use a Poisson model below 40 points and a normal approximation above it, because a 160-point basketball total cannot be summed as a Poisson.

Market-implied. Where no official statistics exist, the only honest source is the OLBG quote. Its reciprocal is printed, the bookmaker margin is left in, and the basis is labelled on every row. It is not our estimate and is not described as one.

3. Grades and the price window

RuleValue

Featured selectionin-band high if one exists, else strongest high, else shortest main line

The window and the confidence floor intersect at 70.00% to 71.43%. That is arithmetic, not a design choice, and it is why the in-band list is short.

4. Estimate bounds

Early-season samples are small, so estimates are held inside the range the sport actually trades in: NHL money lines 24% to 76%, EuroLeague 12% to 88%, MLB 30% to 70%, derived markets 3% to 97%. A team that is 8-1 after nine games has not earned a −3800 price, and the desk will not print one.

5. Predictions

Only high-confidence picks with official statistics get a write-up. Each must be at least 30 words, open with a unique sentence, name the selection, and quote at least two figures that appear on the linked official page. The validator rejects any write-up containing the words "model" or "verified", any duplicate opening, and any fact that is not present in the stored record for that match.

6. What the desk will not do

It will not fill a blank with an inference, quote a price it did not compute or receive, call a market-implied number our own, assert an injury without an official report, or present a started match as an upcoming pick.

Research, not betting advice. Prices shown as "our price" are our own estimates built from the linked official records; prices shown as "OLBG quote" are quoted by OLBG and reproduce the bookmaker margin. 18+.
