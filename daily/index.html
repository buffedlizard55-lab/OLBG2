How to read this desk

All Matches & Lines shows every originated market (match winner, handicap/spread, totals, plus darts leg markets and the F1 race-winner outright). Top 5 per Sport ranks each sport's five highest-probability primary picks. Where a sport has fewer than five verifiable fixtures the slots stay blank and the gap is flagged — quotas are never filled with invented matches.

Prices are this project's own originated fair no-vig lines (decimal = 1/p), not bookmaker quotes and not betting advice. Confidence: high = model probability ≥70% with fair American odds inside −250…+10000 plus a sample gate. Unverified values are left blank and flagged. 18+ Gamble responsibly.
