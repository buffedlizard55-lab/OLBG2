Methodology

Scope

The desk covers exactly the 15 sports requested: Golf, Volleyball, Rugby Union, Cricket, Snooker, Darts, Baseball, Gaelic Sports, Greyhounds, Tennis, Motor Racing, Handball, Ice Hockey, Basketball and Rugby League.

Sources

OLBG is discovery provenance only. Fixture facts, standings, form and injury context are taken from official governing-body pages captured in this session. Every row links to its official source; values that cannot be confirmed are left blank and flagged.

Line origination

Prices are the desk’s own fair lines. For a two-way selection with probability p, fair decimal = 1/p and fair American = −100·p/(1−p) for p ≥ 0.5, otherwise 100·(1−p)/p. No bookmaker quote is published as the desk’s line. Offered decimal equals the desk’s fair line.

Confidence

High: p ≥ 0.70. Medium: 0.60 ≤ p < 0.70. Low: p < 0.60. Small-sample band lines are capped at medium even when p ≥ 0.70.

Odds band

The band is fair American odds from −250 to +10000. Because p ≥ 70% implies fair odds of roughly −233 to −250, only alternate handicap/total lines in that narrow window qualify as in-band plays.

Predictions

Written predictions are produced only for shortlisted high-confidence selections. Each is at least 30 words, has a unique opening, names its selection, and quotes only facts present in the committed fixture capture with an official source link. The words “model” and “verified” do not appear in reader-facing prose.

Every sourced row links to the official governing-body page it came from. Values that an official source did not publish are left blank and listed on Flagged for review. Prices are the desk’s own fair lines, never observed bookmaker quotes unless a row says so.
