Observed OLBG platform defects

  • US egress to hub pages returns “No tips found” instead of an event table.
  • Most OLBG event pages show “WIN @” with blank odds to non-UK visitors.
  • Tip counts are concatenated as strings in the “Most Popular Tip” pill (e.g. “4 / 042”).
  • Unstarted fixtures can show false “Expired” badges with US-Eastern clock drift.

These defects mean the OLBG list is used only for discovery; every priced row in /summit/ carries an official governing-body source.

Every sourced row links to the official governing-body page it came from. Values that an official source did not publish are left blank and listed on Flagged for review. Prices are the desk’s own fair lines, never observed bookmaker quotes unless a row says so.
