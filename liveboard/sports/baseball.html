Predictions

Milwaukee Brewers to win

low TB Rays @ MIL Brewers

Milwaukee bring the best record in the majors into Game 1 of the World Series, having gone 103-59 with a +214 run differential, and they closed the regular season on a five-game winning streak and an 8-2 run over their last ten. That form was built at American Family Field, where they went 55-26. Tampa Bay were excellent too at 98-64, but they arrive off a loss and were a far more ordinary 43-38 on the road. Back the Brewers to take the opener in front of their own crowd.

Over 8.5 runs

low TB Rays @ MIL Brewers

Two deep line-ups meet a pair of pitching staffs that were stingy all summer, yet the arithmetic still points above eight and a half. Milwaukee scored 832 runs and conceded 618 across 162 games, while Tampa Bay put up 736 and gave back 650, which works out at roughly 8.9 combined runs per game when these attacks meet these defences. Both clubs also played the bulk of their schedule under lights, Tampa Bay going 63-35 at night and Milwaukee 68-35, so the evening start at American Family Field is familiar territory. Take the over.

Tampa Bay Rays +1.5

medium TB Rays @ MIL Brewers

Tampa Bay's 98 wins were not the product of blowouts. They went 25-14 in one-run games during the regular season, a .641 record built on exactly the kind of tight games this market is about, and 8-6 in extra innings. That profile is exactly what a +1.5 run line wants: even when the Rays come out on the wrong side, they tend to stay inside a single run. Milwaukee's +214 differential is real, but it does not erase how narrowly Tampa Bay lose.
