Cleanroom Mathematical Origination & Verification Rules

Origination Models, Market Coverage & Confidence Grading

Fair probabilities and no-vig decimal prices (1 / p) are computed deterministically in scripts/verified_model.py using only Python's standard library and official-input registers. No bookmaker price, OLBG tipster consensus, or third-party rating enters an equation. The active output contains 92 originated rows across 7 sports and 74 deliberately unlined rows.

1. Ice Hockey — 19 Originated Rows

Twelve NHL, six SHL and one Liiga fixture use eight-game empirical-Bayes shrinkage of official GF/GA toward a scoring baseline, a Poisson score grid, and a 52% home share of tied regulation outcomes for two-way moneyline. Puck line -1.5/+1.5 and 5.5/6.5 totals are also priced. Liiga's baseline uses only JYP/HPK because a complete current league table was not captured.

2. Basketball — 23 Originated Rows

Fourteen NBA games use ten-game shrinkage of completed official 2025–26 win records, a win-rate logit and normal spread model. Every NBA pick is Medium-capped and every total is blank. HOU@SAS has zero home-court adjustment at neutral Moody Center. Nine current EuroLeague games use three-game PF/PA shrinkage, 3.4-point home advantage, moneyline, spread and totals; expired EuroCup rows are removed.

3. Rugby Union — 23 Originated Rows

Seven Top 14, eight Pro D2 and eight URC Round 4 fixtures use three-match PF/PA shrinkage, 4.2-point home advantage and normal scoring distributions (margin SD 12.6; total SD 14.2). Markets are 1X2 match result, half-point handicap and total points. The Six Nations future remains unlined.

4. Handball — Five Originated Rows

Only the five current EHF Champions League fixtures are priced. Three-match GF/GA shrinkage and a Poisson grid produce 1X2, handicap and total-goal lines. Fifteen other visible domestic/international cards remain unlined pending exact official fixture and ratings confirmation.

5. Darts — 16 Originated Rows

Official PDC ranking prize sums enter a log-ratio leg-strength model; an exact race-to-six distribution prices match winner, leg handicap and 9.5 total legs. PDC inputs are official, while fixture/date labels remain OLBG-sourced. Two outrights are unlined.

6. Baseball & Motor Racing — Six Originated Rows

Three official World Series games use team season run rates in a Poisson model for moneyline, run line and totals. Formula 1 uses official-standing points raised to 1.65 with an explicit 8% residual, a race market, title market, and the actual without-Antonelli market. The US race is 25 October at 16:00 track time; standings retain their stored 21 October capture caveat.

7. Unlined Markets — 74 Rows

Tennis has 20 current cards but zero originated lines; Snooker, Greyhounds, Aussie Rules and eSports have zero active rows. Volleyball, cricket, golf, rugby league and Gaelic football are catalogued with blank markets where exact fixtures, final fields or comparable current official inputs are insufficient. NBA totals and not-applicable outright handicap/total cells are also blank.

8. Strict High Confidence Gate

High requires both: published p > 0.70 and fair American -250 <= odds <= +10000. American prices are derived from the same probability: favourites -100p/(1-p), underdogs +100(1-p)/p. Exactly 70% is not High; -250 and +10000 are inclusive. High also requires a unique official-fact narrative of at least 30 words.

9. Medium, Low & Explicit Caps

Medium applies from 60% upward when the High gate is not passed, including strong favourites shorter than -250, and to every NBA pick regardless of probability. Low applies below 60%. Each pick stores its fair American price and both gate booleans in matches.json; the table exposes blocked High cases.
