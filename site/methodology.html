Methodology.

How independent lines, confidence and high-confidence narratives would be produced — and why none are published in this snapshot.

PRICING FRAMEWORK

Originating our own lines

Requirements before any line is shown

An originated money line, handicap (puck line / point spread) and total (over/under) requires: (1) a documented statistical method (e.g., Elo, Poisson, Dixon-Coles for soccer-free sports), (2) explicit inputs (form, injuries, home advantage) with source links, (3) timestamp, and (4) reproducible calculation (code or spreadsheet). Without these, the table shows — / — / —.

Current status: 00 lines originated. Rule enforced by validation.
Markets examined per match

Every available market is considered: Win match / Money line (2-way incl. OT for NHL, 3-way where applicable), Handicap / Puck line / Point spread (typically ±1.5 in NHL, varies elsewhere), Match total / Game totals Over-Under. OLBG tags are used as research categories only, never as our price.

Examples on OLBG: PHI@BUF Over 5.50; WAS -4.50 spread; Fernandez Win Match.
Winner & confidence

After originating lines, a winner (or Over/Under) is selected and labeled low / medium / high confidence. Low/medium can be a naked pick with a source note. High confidence additionally requires a 30+ word human narrative naming teams, players, current form and recent match history — each factual claim backed by an official source.

Test enforces: confidence high ⇒ winner + 30-word prediction present.
What verification looks like

Line-by-line: every table row has an official league URL (NHL gamecenter, NBA games, MLB schedule, etc.) and an OLBG URL. If a value cannot be confirmed on the official source, it stays blank and the row is flagged. Nothing is inferred. See Flagged page for the current gaps.

Verification log is in COORDINATION.md with direct links.
EXCLUSIONS

Sports outside scope

Excluded by brief

Soccer, boxing, UFC/MMA, football (including Gaelic & American football), hurling, horse racing, cycling, specials. These are never treated as eligible, even if OLBG lists them.

OLBG categories exist for these but are out of scope for this project.

Eligible — but not necessarily covered today

All other sports OLBG tips are in scope when they appear: ice hockey (NHL, EHL, SHL...), basketball (NBA, EuroLeague...), tennis (ATP/WTA/ITF), baseball (MLB), rugby, darts, snooker, volleyball, etc. Coverage depends on what official schedule can verify on the snapshot date.

See category pages for per-sport status.
