Method and scope disclosures (6)

ItemDetail
Odds-band arithmeticThe brief asks for high-confidence picks priced between -250 and +10000. A selection with a 70% chance prices at -233; anything at or above 70% therefore sits between -233 and -10000, and the overlap with a -250 floor is only 70.0%-71.4%. This desk publishes the true model price and marks band membership, rather than moving a line to manufacture compliant prices.
Confidence is a model labelHigh, medium and low describe how far the model's probability sits from 50%. They are not strike rates, guarantees, or a claim that a price is a value bet.
Predictions are team-levelNo injury, lineup, suspension or player-scoring claim is made. Player-level facts were not read on an official page for these fixtures this pass.
Prices are not bookmaker pricesEvery price here is a fair, no-margin price computed as 1 / probability from the model. No bookmaker quote was read or compared.
Two sports cannot reach five fixturesBaseball published two fixtures (World Series games 1 and 2) and cricket one inside the 23-25 October window, so five distinct matches do not exist for those sports here.
Repeated fixtures in the five-pick listsBasketball has three fixtures in the window and rugby union's best five selections include two markets on Cardiff v Sharks. Where a sport has fewer than five fixtures, the list uses additional markets on the same fixture and says so.
