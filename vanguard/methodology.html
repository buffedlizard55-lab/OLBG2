The band

High confidence requires 0.7000 ≤ p ≤ 5/7 = 0.714285… and fair American odds between −250 and +10000. Fair −250 is p = 0.714286, so the window is 1.43 percentage points wide. The gate is searched across every originated market and both sides, and the alternate-line grids are deliberate: a handicap or total can be chosen so a genuine 70.7% opinion lands inside the window, which a money line almost never does.

Reproducibility

Prices are decimal = 1 / p with p rounded to four decimals first, then the American price derived from the rounded decimal. Round-trip a row's probability through the formula in scripts/vanguard_verify.py and you get the printed numbers exactly.
