Top seed against a deciding-leg survivor

Ross Smith · high

Ross Smith to beat Damon Heta in the second round in Dortmund. Smith is the top seed on the 2026 European Tour Rankings with £158,000 banked and opened with a 6-4 win over Karel Sedlacek. Heta needed a deciding leg to get past James Wade 6-5 in round one. The seeding and the cleaner opening round put Smith at about 72 per cent, priced at -257 on the money line.
