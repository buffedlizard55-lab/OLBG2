Methodology

Simple public formulas, exact official inputs, explicit abstention. No OLBG tipster share is treated as a probability.

Two-way log5

p(A beats B) = (a − ab) / (a + b − 2ab).

  • NBA: official 2025-26 away-road and home-home win fractions, 41 games per split.
  • NHL: exact points / (2 × games), not rounded percentage strings.
  • MLB: official 162-game overall records.
  • EuroLeague: official wins / games after Round 7; detailed-evidence gate closed.

Ranking-value share

p(player 1) = rating 1 / (rating 1 + rating 2), with the complement for player 2. Used for ATP ranking points, PDC world-ranking pounds and same-format ICC ODI ratings. Ranking-only rows are capped below high; missing ratings remain blank.

NHL totals & puck lines

Expected goals combine official scoring and opponent allowance per game. Independent Poisson marginals are truncated at 40 and normalized. A regulation tie adds one goal. Only observed 6.5 and 1.5 lines on the two OLBG-linked games are priced; these extra markets remain low confidence.

Fair American price

Favorite: −100p/(1−p). Underdog/even: 100(1−p)/p. Fair decimal is 1/p. Rounded to a whole American unit. These are originated prices, never current bookmaker quotes.

High-confidence gate

  1. Estimated chance strictly greater than 70%.
  2. Fair American between −250 and +10000 inclusive.
  3. Detailed official matchup/statistical evidence and dated availability review where a standard official report exists.
  4. Unique, natural prediction of at least 30 words with an explicit selection and official source links.

Rows meeting only the arithmetic condition stay capped and receive a CAP-… flag. Medium starts at 60%; lower priced estimates are low.

Provenance and blanks

Active on OLBG now requires a current active offer; there are none. Last seen on OLBG earlier today includes expired direct pages and the 16:20 discovery inventory. Official context only means no individual OLBG event was observed. Official sources control fixture facts; OLBG supplies discovery only.

Every blank row states why no price is supportable and appears in the review queue. No quota, injury, timezone, rating or market value is manufactured.
