Our own numbers,
and their limits.

No bookmaker price is copied, scraped or used as an input. Every published price is recomputed from the official inputs stored with the fixture.

1. What counts as a verified fixture

A row is published only when the competition's own feed returns the two teams, the fixture date and the start time. For this snapshot that means the NHL game-centre payload at api-web.nhle.com, plus the NHL game page for each fixture. OLBG is recorded as the coverage reference the brief asked for, but OLBG is never treated as an official match source.

2. The model

lambda_home = ((home.GF/GP + away.GA/GP) / 2) × 1.05 lambda_away = ((away.GF/GP + home.GA/GP) / 2) × 0.95 P(i, j) = Poisson(i; lambda_home) × Poisson(j; lambda_away), i,j = 0..10

Goals for and against per game come from the official payload. The blend is a standard attack-versus-defence estimate: each side's scoring rate is averaged with the opponent's concession rate.

3. Markets

  • Money line (two-way, overtime and shootout included): P(home win) + 0.5 × P(tie). The site's own model assumes the extra period is a coin flip; that assumption is stated, not hidden.
  • Puck line 1.5: P(margin ≥ 2) for the favourite, and its complement for the underdog.
  • Match total: over and under at 5.5 and 6.5 goals, read from the same 60-minute matrix.
  • Regulation three-way: shown for transparency; it is not the market the selection is taken from.

4. Prices and confidence

Prices are fair decimal odds, 1 / probability, rounded to two decimals. No margin is added, so these are not sportsbook prices and should not be read as ones.

Confidence is a property of this model, not a claim about the real world. The selected side is always the side the model prices shorter. The label then comes from how far that side sits from a coin flip:

LabelConditionMeaning
highedge ≥ 10 pointsThe model separates the sides by at least 20 points of probability.
medium6–10 pointsA moderate model edge.
lowunder 6 pointsClose to a coin flip; published for completeness only.

5. Narratives

Only high-confidence selections carry a written prediction, and each is at least 30 words. Every factual statement in a narrative maps to a field in the official payload: goals for and against per game, points percentage, power-play and penalty-kill rank, the last-ten record, the listed results, the head coach or the schedule. Nothing is added from memory.

6. Model parameters are assumptions

The home multiplier (1.05), away multiplier (0.95), tie split (0.5) and the confidence edges are choices made by this desk. They are not league statistics and are not presented as verified facts. They live in data/events.json under model, so anyone can change them and re-run scripts/build.py to reproduce every price.

7. What the model does not do

  • It does not adjust for rest, back-to-backs, travel or schedule density.
  • It does not know who starts in goal, who is injured, scratched or suspended.
  • It does not use shots, expected goals, special-teams match-up or score effects.
  • It does not shrink small samples. Early-season rates are carried in exactly as published, which is why some prices look extreme.
  • It does not compare against market prices, so it cannot claim value against a bookmaker.

8. Reproduction

python -m unittest discover -s tests -v python scripts/build.py python -m http.server 8000 -d site

The build refuses to run if a row lacks an approved source host, if a selection disagrees with the model, if a high-confidence narrative is under 30 words or fails to name its selection, or if the coverage board's row counts disagree with the fixture list.
