Method

How a pick gets on this page.

1 · Listing

Each of the 15 sport hubs is read directly. The event, its displayed time, the most-tipped selection, the tip count and the tip share are copied verbatim into the OLBG list. OLBG publishes no odds on hub pages, so nothing here is copied from an OLBG price.

2 · Official confirmation

A fixture is only publishable once a governing body or league feed confirmed the teams and the start time. A pick additionally requires a corresponding event in the captured OLBG board; an official-only fixture may have research prices but is not promoted to the pick card. Tennis not-before times are labelled as earliest starts, not exact beginnings. The sport-specific evidence is on Flagged for review.

3 · Our line

NHL and MLB moneylines use official record strength: probability = 0.5 + home edge + 0.85 × (home strength − away strength), clamped to 12–88%. Home edge is 4.5 points for the NHL and 4.0 for MLB. NBA moneylines combine the completed 2025-26 record with current results and ten pseudo-games of .500 shrinkage, then use the same 0.85 strength scale and a four-point home edge. ATP and Ningbo use comparable official season records with ten pseudo-matches; Osaka uses only current-tournament match and completed-set records because comparable season records were not available.

Volleyball blends one-round match, set and point strengths with four matches, twelve sets and 600 points of shrinkage. Handball uses W-L-D strength to calibrate a seven-goal-standard-deviation margin and shrunken scoring rates for a regular-time Poisson matrix, so the tie remains a separate outcome.

Darts leg probability combines 0.08 times the world-earnings log ratio, 0.06 times the European Tour earnings log ratio and 0.035 times the difference in tournament leg differential. Exact first-to-ten outcomes produce match winner, both 2.5-leg handicaps and the 16.5 total. Saturday match times are OLBG display values inside the official PDC session window.

Formula 1 uses each current starter's points base (0.15 + points / maximum points), season-win factor (1 + wins / maximum wins) and Practice 2 factor (1 / (1 + gap seconds)). Weights normalize across all 22 starters for race-winner prices. Every ordered top-three outcome is enumerated exactly for podium prices. Qualifying and grid position are not inputs.

NHL and MLB scoring markets use independent Poisson score matrices from official goals/runs for and against; NHL rates receive five league-average games of shrinkage and a 0.15-goal home adjustment. NBA scoring rates receive ten 114-point games of shrinkage. Spread margin is calibrated to the same moneyline probability with a 12-point standard deviation, so early scoring cannot reverse the favourite; totals use an 18-point standard deviation. All NBA lines are half-points. Every fair American price is calculated from its stated probability, not copied from OLBG or a bookmaker.

4 · Confidence and the band

High is 70% or better, medium 55–69.95%, low below 55%. The band test checks only our own fair price against −250…+10000. Because −250 is 71.43%, a "70% or better" rule and that band overlap only between 70.00% and 71.43%. Every price outside the overlap keeps its calculated number and an explicit outside-band label.

5 · The writing

Every preview states the selection first, then gives match-specific form, numbers and caveats. Figures in the prose appear in the fact list under that preview with official links. NBA injuries come only from the league's 1:30 PM ET report. NOT YET SUBMITTED stays unresolved. In every other sport without an official injury or availability report read in this session, the field remains blank and says so.

6 · What is deliberately absent

No price is labelled as available at a bookmaker. No fixture is invented to fill a five-per-sport quota. No value is inferred where a source was silent.
