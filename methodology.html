Methodology

How stored inputs are transformed into model lines and labels, and what the checks do and do not establish. This documents the model; it does not claim each source page or sporting fact was independently re-verified.

1. Scope

Included: Tennis, Basketball, Golf, Cricket, Snooker, Rugby Union, Rugby League, Baseball, Ice Hockey, Motor Racing, Darts, Handball, Volleyball, Aussie Rules, eSports, Gaelic Football, Greyhounds.

Excluded by the brief: football (soccer, OLBG id 1), horse racing (2), American football (11), boxing (16), cycling (17) and hurling (26). UFC/MMA have no separate OLBG sport id — they are filed under Boxing/16, so the exclusion of id 16 covers them. "Specials" are not a sport on OLBG; they are a bet-type, and no specials market is included anywhere. The list is enforced in scripts/config.py.

Two scope judgement calls, both flagged: Gaelic Football is treated as a distinct sport and kept (the brief excludes "football", which we read as soccer); hurling is excluded by name.

2. What OLBG gives us, and what it does not

Sport hub pages supply the event list, the event_id, the market label a tipster used, and tip percentages. They do not publish prices. Nothing in this dataset uses OLBG tip percentages as a probability: our lines are originated independently. OLBG is only an event-discovery and market-vocabulary source.

3. Evidence and verification limits

Rows include source links captured for this dated snapshot, but the presence of a URL is not evidence that its contents were checked or that it confirms every displayed field. A later cross-layer audit performed only a limited NHL schedule spot-check; it did not freshly verify every fixture, strength input, market, or prediction. The build validates internal structure and source-link presence, not external page contents. Refer to the evidence-status review and the review queue; treat any fact without explicit confirmation as unverified rather than assuming that the linked page supports it.

4. The models

Ice Hockey (NHL)

A multiplicative Poisson attack/defence model on the goals-per-game inputs stored in this snapshot, with empirical-Bayes shrinkage. The underlying statistics are not freshly revalidated by this audit.

league_avg = mean(goals for per game) over all 32 teams
w          = gp / (gp + 8.0)
attack_i   = 1 + w * ((gf_i / gp_i) / league_avg - 1)
defence_i  = 1 + w * ((ga_i / gp_i) / league_avg - 1)
lambda_home = league_avg * attack_home * defence_away * 1.05
lambda_away = league_avg * attack_away * defence_home / 1.05
P(home)    = Poisson score grid, tie split 50/50 (overtime / shootout)
puck line  = fixed at 1.5, P(home covers) = P(margin >= 2)
total line = nearest half-point to lambda_home + lambda_away

Home-ice factor 1.05 and shrinkage k=8.0 are our own line-setting parameters, not sourced facts. They are recorded in every NHL row.

Basketball (NBA)

The model uses stored 2025-26 records as a prior, shrunk 25% toward .500. The underlying standings values and the description of the current season are not freshly revalidated by this audit.

raw      = wins / (wins + losses)            # 2025-26
shrunk   = 0.75 * raw + 0.25 * 0.5
rating   = logit(shrunk)
margin   = 11.0 * (rating_home + 0.2 - rating_away)
P(home)  = logistic(margin / 11.0)
handicap = nearest half-point to -margin
total    = NOT ORIGINATED (no verified points-for/against input was captured)

Rugby Union (URC)

The checked-in model input represents points for and against per game from a league table, shrunk toward the league mean. The source page contents and values were not freshly revalidated in this copy audit.

w = pl / (pl + 6.0)
attack_i  = 1 + w * ((pf_i / pl_i) / league_pf - 1)
defence_i = 1 + w * ((pa_i / pl_i) / league_pa - 1)
expected_home = league_pf * attack_home * defence_away + 3.0/2
expected_away = league_pa * attack_away * defence_home - 3.0/2
P(home)  = logistic((expected_home - expected_away) / 13.0)
handicap = nearest half-point to the expected margin
total    = expected_home + expected_away, nearest half-point

Prices

Every price is round(1 / (p * 1.04), 2), clamped to 1.05–12.00. That is a fair price plus a 4% theoretical margin. These are not bookmaker prices and were not compared to any bookmaker.

Handball (EHF Champions League Men)

The model uses the stored group-phase goals-for and goals-against inputs, shrinks them toward its competition mean, then converts the estimates into a joint Poisson score grid. The source values and round count are not freshly revalidated by this copy audit:

league_gf  = sum(goals for) / sum(games played) over all 24 clubs
w          = gp / (gp + 3.0)
attack_i   = 1 + w * ((gf_i / gp_i) / league_gf - 1)
defence_i  = 1 + w * ((ga_i / gp_i) / league_ga - 1)
exp_home   = league_gf * attack_home * defence_away + 0.8 / 2
exp_away   = league_gf * attack_away * defence_home - 0.8 / 2
P(result)  = sum of Poisson(i; exp_home) * Poisson(j; exp_away) over the matching scores

Match winner is three-way (draw included). Handicap uses a half-goal line set just beyond the expected margin, and match total uses a half-goal line just below the expected total, so no bet can push. Prices apply a 4% theoretical margin to the model probability; no bookmaker price is used anywhere. A handball pick is graded high only when the model gives it at least 70% and both clubs have played four or more group games, medium from 58%, and low below that.
