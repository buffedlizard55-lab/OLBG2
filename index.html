Analysis

These figures are computed from the published dataset itself, not from any external claim.

Coverage and evidence

  • 112 of 155 rows (72%) carry originated prices — moneyline and handicap on every lined row, plus totals wherever the official feed publishes scoring data; 43 rows are blank with a stated reason.
  • 92 of 155 rows cite a URL that identifies the individual fixture; the remaining 63 cite a competition or rankings page, disclosed as VF-11.
  • 15 rows were re-read on 2026-10-22; the other 140 keep their 2026-10-21 capture stamp.
  • The snapshot spans 47 competition labels in 15 sports; Aussie Rules and eSports returned no active fixtures and are shown empty rather than skipped.

Prices and confidence

  • Confidence split: 20 high, 38 medium, 54 low, 43 unlined/no pick.
  • 13 band picks sit at fair odds 1.40-1.42 with model probabilities of 70.0-71.4%, the only window satisfying both the ≥70% rule and the −250 floor.
  • The 20 original high-confidence picks are priced 1.02-1.39 and are labelled outside band; they are not presented as meeting the brief's price rule.
  • The longest fair price anywhere in the 33 predictions is 1.42, inside the +10000 ceiling.

What this project does — and does not do

Does

  • Records OLBG's sport taxonomy and excludes the eight categories in the brief.
  • Reads fixture, date, venue and form data from governing bodies or leagues.
  • Originates its own moneyline, handicap and total prices from those inputs.
  • Writes unique, sourced predictions for every published high-confidence pick.
  • Leaves unconfirmed values blank and publishes them for review.

Does not

  • Claim a live, exhaustive OLBG schedule — the capture is dated 2026-10-22 01:30 UTC.
  • Copy bookmaker odds or tipster percentages into the model.
  • Fill a missing venue, ranking or total with an inferred value.
  • Guarantee any selection; confidence is an estimate, not certainty.
  • Present past fixtures as upcoming.
