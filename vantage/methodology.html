OUR ESTIMATES / PRICE & CONFIDENCE RULES

How a row becomes a written selection

The first OLBG page is a discovery list, not a complete fixture feed or an odds provider. An individual match only gets our own fair line after its league confirms the exact participants and a future official start, and both sides have comparable numerical input. At 17:50 UTC we withheld starts under 90 minutes away to allow for static-site publishing latency.

01Discover

Keep the OLBG event URL, single most-tipped outcome and original displayed time. Do not convert the unspecific publisher clock to UTC.

02Confirm

Check the pairing and start against a sporting governing body; dates and time zones are displayed as published. Unknown or conflicting fields are blank and reviewed.

03Originate

Use goals or runs for both clubs from a dated league table. Calculations below produce independent, no-vig estimates for win, handicap, total where those inputs exist.

04Write

Every displayed candidate needs 30+ unique human words, a named market/selection, opponent context, current form and direct official links. Unsupported injuries are not guessed.

Fair price is not a quote

p≥50% → American = −round(100 p/(1−p)); below 50% → +round(100 (1−p)/p). Estimated lines are no-vig and omit bookmaker margin.

Grade ≠ guarantee

High if an eligible NHL match-winner estimate is ≥70%; medium if ≥58%; otherwise low. EHF and MLB high estimates are capped at medium, and short-sample rugby at low. These rules are editorial, not externally calibrated.

Band is a second check

Only −250…+10000 American is inside the fair-price focus. For an executive pick we require high grade, written prose and a separate in-band observed quote. OLBG's current offered price remains unconfirmed.

Known limits: sport hubs only expose their first page; no five-per-sport quota can be met on unsupported events. Injury fields require a dated, event-specific official report; the NHL, EHF, MLB and Top 14 rows shown here do not imply an injury-free lineup. Once a fixture starts, a static page cannot place a bet or re-check its status.
