REVERSE ENGINEERING

OLBG structure observed

OLBG is a tipster hub, not an odds provider

OLBG aggregates community tips per event (event_id). Each page lists tipster selections, win-tip percentages, and market tags (Money Line, Puck Line / Point Spread, Game Totals / Over-Under). It does not originate prices.

Examples: Ice hockey index and PHI@BUF / FLA@OTT pages list Over 5.50 at 00:37 local display; Basketball shows MIL@WAS Money Line 9/11 82% with Pointspread -4.50 and Under 239.50. OLBG tips hub ↗

NHL gamecenters consistently say 7:30 PM EDT for both Oct 21 games. OLBG shows 19:37 / 00:37 without a stated timezone, so no reconciliation is inferred. This is published as an irregularity on the Flagged page.

Markets considered, not priced

For NHL, the club examines Money line (2-way including OT), Puck line (usually ±1.5), and Game total (Over/Under). Without a documented model, inputs, timestamp and reproducible calculation, no independent line is published — hence the — / — / — display.
