Band arithmetic

A price of −250 is 71.4% implied. A selection that is both above 70% and no shorter than −250 can therefore only sit in the 70.00–71.43% window. 22 selections grade high; 8 are inside the band and all of those are alternate lines quoted at the ceiling. The other 14 are real prices shorter than −250 and are published with their true numbers rather than hidden. The band page sets out the arithmetic.

What this desk does not claim

Not a live feed

The OLBG pages were read once, at 20:32Z. Prices move. A price shown here is our fair estimate plus a standard margin, not a quote from a bookmaker.

Not full coverage

93 fixtures are priced out of 127 upcoming OLBG events. Where there was no defensible input the row is blank and the reason is printed, rather than a number being invented.

Not independent of the publisher

Fixture listings come from OLBG; the facts behind each price come from the competition's own site. Where a fact could not be joined to a stored official body, the row says so on Verification.

Not a tip service

High confidence is a statement about our own probability, not about the outcome. 22 of 122 graded selections clear 70%.

What this desk is. A dated, source-linked research record of sport listed on OLBG, of our own originated prices, and of confidence-graded selections. It is not a live odds feed and it does not place bets.

Every price, probability and confidence label is our own estimate, derived from the official statistics named on the row. Values that could not be confirmed are left blank and listed on Flagged for review. Capture times and source files are printed on every table.
