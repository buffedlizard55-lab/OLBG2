Overview / Five per sport

The day's card, five per sport at most

The brief asks for up to five high-confidence picks per sport per day and says that is a maximum, not a quota. This page applies the ceiling literally. Selections inside the −250 to +10000 window come first, then the price nearest that window, then the shortest price, then the earliest start.

Where the card is short. Only three sports carry official per-match statistics in the captured evidence, so most sports cannot produce a statistically earned high-confidence pick. Rather than promote a short-priced favourite into the slot, the shortfall is printed under each sport and repeated on Predictions and Flagged for Review.
