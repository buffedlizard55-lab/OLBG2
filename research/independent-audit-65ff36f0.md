# Independent publication integrity audit — 2026-10-22 UTC

**Scope and distinction.** This is an independent read-only scan of five already-published overlapping layers (`verified`, `hub`, `slate`, `board`, `forward`) plus three targeted primary-source comparisons. The script does not retrieve live data or substantiate every statistical assertion. The official pages below were opened/retrieved separately during this session using the web retrieval tools. An HTTPS publisher hostname is *necessary but not sufficient* evidence that a row's fixture, line, or prose is correct. No upstream match, prediction, or generated dashboard file was changed, because those scopes were owned by earlier sessions; requested corrections are recorded in `COORDINATION.md`.

## Methods and result

`python3 scripts/audit65.py` reads every committed row in the five `data/<layer>/matches.json` snapshots, checks whether its `official_url` has HTTPS and an identified governing-body/operator hostname, then reads every featured prediction to check its publisher hostname and 30-word minimum. It also compares shared IDs for conflicting away participants. It emits `data/audit65/report.json` and a two-page static evidence desk at `audit65/`. The layers overlap and must not be added to obtain a unique-fixture count. **The audit does not claim that all rows were verified against original match pages.** Hostname classification is structural; market prices are model outputs, not independently verifiable official results.

- `verified`: 128 fixture rows / 14 featured predictions (10 GBGB greyhound fixtures were added by PR #78 while this audit was integrating).
- `hub`: 189 / 25.
- `slate`: 148 / 36 (plus 23 unlined fixtures in a separate `unlined_fixtures` field, outside this scan).
- `board`: 207 / 35.
- `forward`: 93 / 8.
- Totals *with overlaps*: **765 row instances**, **118 prediction instances**; 15 source/date/participant findings (8 eSports + 3 golf + 4 darts), 1 featured prediction with non-primary `official_url` (`lol-w26-r1-1`), zero featured predictions under 30 words. Exact details are in the generated report and the site review table.

### A65-DARTS — PDC participant dispute (4 published layer instances)

Source reads: [PDC 19 Oct schedule](https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/october/19/2026-machineseeker-european-championship-schedule-confirmed/) lists `Danny Noppert v Stephen Bunting` in Thursday's first round. [PDC 21 Oct official preview](https://www.pdc.tv/news/2026/october/21/2026-machineseeker-european-championship-preview) also lists `Danny Noppert v Stephen Bunting`; the preview's indexed official text says the Dutch player *meets Stephen Bunting*, and its schedule/draw gives the same pairing. **Retrieval limit:** The PDC page reader returned a giant cookie-consent overlay and did not expose the article body directly in its text chunks; this comparison relies on search-indexed text from the *official PDC URLs*, not a post-21-Oct live draw. A newer draw may supersede these snapshots; a Bunting withdrawal was **not confirmed** by an accessible official article during this session. Therefore neither participant should be claimed definitively current without a fresh official match-centre check.

`data/slate/matches.json`, `data/slate/predictions.json` (`rank: 9`, `match_id: pdc-32330`) and `data/slate/flagged.json` (`SL-01`) instead assert that Bunting withdrew, Edhouse replaced him, and the 21 Oct preview *confirmed* it. That citation does not support the asserted replacement in its indexed official text. A prediction recommending Danny Noppert against Edhouse is therefore **not independently verified**. Three other layers (`verified`, `hub`, `board`) retain Bunting; their 19–21 Oct basis is older, not proof of the latest draw. **Disposition:** leave slate replacement value blank in this review, request the slate owner remove/suspend that selection until a later official result/draw is supplied. Do not silently switch contestants based on either snapshot.

The slate layer had already flagged it (`SL-02`) but the other dashboards had not reconciled it. **Disposition:** suppress old row in its owners' originating data, then regenerate the owned dashboards. Do not infer a new Bermuda outright market or participants from the event date alone.

### A65-ES — wiki citations for eight eSports entries (8 published layer instances)

`data/slate/matches.json` (`lol-w26-r1-1` through `-8`) cites `liquipedia.net` as `official_url` and for form/qualification sources; the one featured prediction `lol-w26-r1-1` also cites Liquipedia as official. Liquipedia is community-edited, not the competition operator. The [Riot Games-operated LoL Esports official schedule](https://lolesports.com/en-US/) rendered the eight Worlds 2026 Swiss Round 1 pairings under `Tomorrow` on 22 October: Gen.G–Invictus Gaming; Hanwha Life–TOP ESPORTS; BILIBILI GAMING–Movistar KOI; Anyone's Legend–Karmine Corp; G2–Dplus KIA; Team Liquid Alienware–CTBC Flying Oyster; LOS–T1; Team Secret Whales–LYON. [Riot Worlds tournament page](https://lolesports.com/en-US/tournament/115660540725177488) also labels the competition. Team Liquid and Secret Whales are abbreviated in the slate; the T1 pairing's order is reversed. All eight pairings appear on Riot's schedule, but **the slate's quoted prices, form/qualification details and prediction prose were not line-by-line corroborated on Riot's site**. Do not call them official-source verified on the basis of a Liquipedia citation. **Disposition:** the source-owner should replace each match citation with Riot's own match/schedule URL, inspect alias and team order where material, and independently verify each model input before restoring a verified badge. On the review page, unsupported exact display names/order are left blank for rows 6–8.
