RESEARCH RULES

Verification methodology

A conservative framework for building an auditable fixture and prediction dataset. The rules below describe publication criteria; no match has passed them yet.

Evidence hierarchy

Use the official competition/league schedule first for event identity, date, time, participants, venue, and status. Use the governing body, official team/club, or athlete source for rules, roster status, results, and other facts. Link each claim to the specific first-party page that supports it. OLBG is a reference target for site structure only, not the authority for facts about an event.

1 · Confirm scopeCheck sport and event against the requested exclusions before creating a record.
2 · Verify the fixtureRecord participants and start time only when directly confirmed on an official source.
3 · Research formAttach official recent-result sources; do not infer current form from stale or secondary pages.
4 · Audit marketsConfirm market definition and settlement period. Keep unavailable or unconfirmed fields blank.

Line and pick standards

Our line: must be produced by a documented, repeatable method using identified inputs. Record the exact selection/threshold and calculation timestamp. Do not copy an operator price, extrapolate missing odds, or represent a market target as available.

Confidence: low, medium, or high only after a defined calibration policy exists and relevant evidence has been checked. Confidence is not certainty; do not assign it to an unsourced opinion.

High-confidence copy: publish only when all factual details have direct official citations. Each prediction should be unique, natural, at least 30 words, name the team/player and clear selection, and accurately summarize verified current form and recent match history. If any of those claims cannot be checked, publish no prediction.

Current state: no verified fixtures, no line-generation methodology or line values, no confidence ratings, and no prediction copy. This is intentional rather than a placeholder prediction.

Data hygiene

Show when a source was checked; preserve direct links; distinguish a blank/unverified field from zero, unavailable, or no event. Flag stale links, conflicts between official sources, ambiguous exclusions, and any value whose provenance cannot be reproduced. Never claim “complete” coverage without checking the full sport/competition schedule.

Current research scope excludes soccer, boxing, UFC, MMA, football, American football, hurling, horse racing, cycling, and specials. Any uncertain category treatment remains flagged rather than silently included or omitted.
