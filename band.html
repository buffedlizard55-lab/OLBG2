The −250 to +10000 band

The brief asks for high-confidence picks priced between −250 and +10000. Those two conditions interact, and the desk refuses to paper over it.

Arithmetic

A moneyline of −250 implies a 71.43% chance (250 ÷ 350). A moneyline of +10000 implies 0.99%. The band therefore runs from 0.99% up to 71.43%. “High confidence” here means a chance of 70% or more.

The overlap is 70.00–71.43% — a 1.43-point window. A selection at 78% is outside the band however confident it is; a selection at 40% is inside the band while being nowhere near high confidence.

What the desk does

It publishes both, labelled. 22 selections grade high; 8 are inside the band. None of them is inside it on a natural price: all 8 clear it only by quoting an alternate line, marked as such here and in the prose.

The other 14 high selections are shorter than −250. They are not deleted to make the scoreboard look tidier: each is listed below with its true price.
