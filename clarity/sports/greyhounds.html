Written predictions

Shadow Storm can join the four-timer club in the Scurry Gold Cup

high · Sat 24 Oct 18:00 UTC

Shadow Storm · Outright winner · +233

Back Shadow Storm to win the Arena Racing Company Scurry Gold Cup final at Dunstall Park. Richard Rees' dog is the reigning GBGB Sprinter of the Year, holds the track record and clocked 15.66 in his semi-final, the fastest qualifying run over the 270 metres. He is chasing a second straight Scurry and would be only the fourth greyhound to win back-to-back renewals of a £12,500 Category One prize.

Fahrenheit Jess has the clock and the kennel behind her in the Oaks

high · Sat 24 Oct 18:15 UTC

Fahrenheit Jess · Outright winner · +292

Back Fahrenheit Jess to win the Premier Greyhound Racing Oaks final at Dunstall Park. Graham Holland's Irish raider made all in her semi-final in 27.87, the fastest of the three qualifiers, and her trainer also saddles Ballydoyle Tina, so the kennel knows the track shape. Signet Nayla won her semi in 28.35 and Slingshot Poppy in 27.90, which leaves Holland's leading bitch with a clear early-pace edge over the 480 metres for the £20,000 prize.
