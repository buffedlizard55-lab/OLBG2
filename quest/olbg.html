OLBG reverse-engineering map (re-read 2026-10-23, ~21:30 UTC)

Source pages read first-hand tonight: olbg.com/betting-tips and the Basketball tips page.

URL schema

  • Sport tips index: https://www.olbg.com/betting-tips/{Sport}/{sport_id} — for example Tennis is /Tennis/3, Ice Hockey /Ice_Hockey/13.
  • Competition level inserts path segments before the id: /Basketball/NBA/4, /Basketball/European/Euroleague/4, /Basketball/NBA/NBA_Championship/4 (outrights live here too).
  • Gaelic Sports is split by OLBG into Gaelic Football (/Gaelic_Football/25) and Hurling (/Hurling/26); this board covers both under the single mandated “Gaelic Sports” heading.
  • Event pages are reached from the (client-rendered) event tables; a stored example from an earlier capture uses the query form /betting-tips/Ice_Hockey/13?event_id=199984.
  • Tipster rankings mirror the same ids: /best-tipsters/{Sport}/{sport_id}.

How OLBG selections work (from the pages read)

  • Tips come from a free tipster community/competitions (running since 2002, over £1.2m paid out per OLBG's own page copy). Each selection shows tip count, percentage of tips behind it, expert counts (high strike-rate/profit tipsters) and a “Value Rating” comparing odds with tip-share-implied probability.
  • Event pages surface a Most Popular Tip and a Best Tipsters Tip, then every outcome per market with backer counts and written comments.
  • Basketball's three headline markets are named explicitly on its page: Money Line, Pointspread and Game Totals (overtime usually counts). Other sports follow the same families — match winner, handicap, match total — plus outrights; the market families originated on this board per sport are listed below.
Hard limit (flag Q-OLBG1). The event tables render client-side: both pages read tonight returned the literal text “No tips found” to a server-side fetch, even though the sports are in season. A complete OLBG event inventory therefore cannot be captured without a browser. This board reproduces OLBG's taxonomy and market vocabulary, takes fixtures from each governing body instead, and joins to OLBG at sport level on every row. Only 1 of 93 inherited fixtures carries an OLBG event id (an unsupported Gaelic value was removed upstream in PR #178), so no event-level join is trusted yet.
