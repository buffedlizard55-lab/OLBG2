Analysis of this snapshot

Coverage and evidence

  • 339 of 382 rows (89%) carry originated prices; the other 43 are blank with a published reason.
  • 176 rows cite a URL that identifies the individual fixture; 206 cite a competition, rankings or player page. Both are disclosed on every row.
  • The five source layers held 885 rows; de-duplication produced 382 fixtures. No layer is summed into another.
  • 0 excluded-sport rows survived the filter. Aussie Rules keeps nine real fixtures from the slate layer; the eight eSports pairings were only captured on a community wiki, so they are published unlined and unverified.

Prices and confidence

  • Confidence split: 19 high, 171 medium, 149 low, 43 unlined.
  • High means ≥70% model probability and a fair price of 1.40–101.00. Because a 70% shot prices at 1.43, the only overlap is roughly 70.0–71.4%; that is why the high set is small and why 100+ stronger favourites are graded medium with the explicit reason "shorter than −250".
  • The longest high-confidence price is 1.43; the shortest is 1.40.
  • No bookmaker price enters any equation. Where a source layer published synthetic "book" columns, they were dropped.
