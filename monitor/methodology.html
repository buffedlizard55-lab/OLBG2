How the desk works

1. Exact scope, no silent expansion

The 15 requested categories are the only categories in this monitor. Gaelic Sports includes Gaelic Football and Hurling, with no automatic equivalence between the disciplines. The historical 17-sport desks are archived separately.

2. OLBG discovery is not official confirmation

Public hubs expose event links with numeric event_id parameters. The collector extracts those links, deduplicated within a sport. The first-page/load-more inventory is partial; JavaScript pagination and private APIs have not been certified. No access controls are bypassed. An OLBG vote share describes tipster support, not a win probability.

Fixture names, dates, clocks and IDs are checked against the governing body. A generic official page that does not contain a fixture is only a check target. Relative OLBG dates are not converted to UTC. NBA clocks explicitly labelled ET are normalized using America/New_York; all other ambiguous clocks remain unconverted.

3. Source facts versus derived estimates

Only MLB Game 1 has an experimental originated moneyline. For home regular-season win fraction H and away fraction A, P(home) = (H − H×A) / (H + A − 2×H×A). The away probability is 1 − P(home). The inputs are the official 2026 records, 103–59 and 98–64. No bookmaker odds or tip shares enter the calculation.

This log5 baseline assumes comparable opposition and a neutral venue. It is not calibrated and does not account for starters, injuries, postseason form or home advantage. Its confidence is low regardless of the numeric output. No asserted factual values are invented. Run-line and total prices are withheld because appropriate scoring and settlement assumptions have not been validated.

4. Odds band applies to an observed offered price

Fair American odds: −100p/(1−p) above 50%; +100(1−p)/p at or below 50%. Bookmaker American odds are a different field and require a dated quote and source. The requested offered-price band is −250 to +10000. A fair-price-only proxy at p ≥ 70% would restrict p to 70–71.43%, but that is not the same as finding a well-supported selection available at those odds. We never silently substitute the proxy.

5. Low, medium, high

Missing inputs produce no confidence label. Uncalibrated exploratory estimates are low. A future calibrated estimate would be low below 60%, medium from 60% to below 70%, and high from 70%. Publishing a high-confidence recommendation additionally requires a current official scheduled fixture, OLBG match identity, a future UTC start, fresh evidence, an actual price in the band and a dated official evidence packet. No current record passes.

6. Ranking and original writing

Qualifying events are sorted by probability, then UTC start, then stable ID; only one selection per event counts toward the five-per-sport target. Today means UTC at refresh. Upcoming is kept separate from today. Shortfalls stay visible. High-confidence writing must have at least 30 words with match-specific official facts, clear selection, non-repetitive prose and supported recent form/history. Injuries are included only with relevant dated evidence; absence of a report never means everyone is healthy. No high-confidence prose is generated until that evidence exists.

7. Freshness and automation

The Pages workflow runs unattended every six hours. It archives source responses and failures, extracts OLBG event links and parses MLB schedules. Other official fixture adapters and automatic supported prose are not complete. The checked-in seed is never relabelled as a fresh capture. Runtime evidence uses separate timestamps and overrides a matching official fixture without carrying old predictions into it. Failed refreshes leave historical evidence dated and display the failure ledger. The browser marks starts in the past and captures older than six hours, without relying on a frozen “today” date.

8. What tests prove

Tests check arithmetic, nulls, evidence references, filtering, duplicate protection, links, odds boundaries, source integrity and reproducible output. They do not establish sports facts beyond the source extracts. Local links are audited separately from external source access. This work is a selected line/field audit, not a claim to have externally verified every line of all historical data.
