Cleanroom Mathematical Origination & Verification Rules

Origination Models, Market Coverage & Confidence Grading

All fair probabilities and prices (decimal 1 / p with the equivalent American moneyline, zero bookmaker overround) are computed deterministically in scripts/verified_model.py using only the Python standard library and the single self-contained official capture in data/verified/inputs.json, written by scripts/verified_seed.py on 2026-10-22.

1. Ice Hockey (NHL — 12 Games)

Empirical-Bayes shrunk attack/defence goal rates (k = 8 games toward the league mean from the official NHL Stats REST summary, shootout-deciding goals excluded) feeding a full-support bivariate Poisson grid with a 52%/48% home/away OT/SO split. Originates 2-way Moneyline, -1.5/+1.5 Puck Line, and 5.5/6.5 Total Goals.

2. Basketball (NBA 14, EuroLeague 9, EuroCup —)

Opening-week NBA uses completed 2025-26 records as a shrunk win-rate logit prior, confidence capped at MEDIUM, featured write-ups disabled, and totals left blank because nba.com/standings omits PF/PA. EuroLeague Round 7 uses a shrunk efficiency margin normal model (k = 3) across Moneyline, Spread, and Total Points. EuroCup Round 5 was not captured this refresh (flag VF-20).

3. Rugby Union (Top 14 7, Pro D2 8, URC 16)

Normal scoring-margin and total-points distributions with empirical-Bayes shrinkage (k = 3 matches) and +4.2 home advantage across 1X2 Match Result (with exact draw mass), Half-Point Handicap, and Total Points.

4. Handball (EHF Champions League — 5 Matches)

Full-support bivariate Poisson model (k = 3) on the fresh official 24-club standings after the 21 Oct Round 5 games, originating 1X2 Match Winner, Half-Goal Handicap, and Over/Under Total Goals.

5. Darts (PDC European Championship — 16 Ties)

Exact negative-binomial race-to-6-legs calculation from log two-year PDC Werner Ranking Ladder prize sums for all 32 qualifiers, pricing Match Winner, -1.5/+1.5 Leg Handicap, and Over/Under 9.5 Total Legs. The 22 Oct rankings re-read was blocked by the PDC consent wall, so the 21 Oct ladder stands — no ranking event was completed in between (flag VF-21).

6. Tennis & Snooker (Currently Unlined)

Exact best-of-3 sets / best-of-7 frames combinatorial models stand ready, but the 2026-10-22 capture could verify no ATP order of play and none of the eight WST Northern Ireland Open Round 3 ties has both players in the captured ranking input — all are listed blank on Flagged for Review rather than estimated.

7. Baseball & Motor Racing (MLB WS G1–G4, 2 F1 Markets)

World Series Games 1–4 (Tampa Bay Rays 98-64, 736 RS / 650 RA vs Milwaukee Brewers 103-59, 832 RS / 618 RA) use run-rate Poisson with the league rate derived from the two clubs' own verified totals across Moneyline, -1.5/+1.5 Run Line, and 7.5 Total Runs; if-necessary Games 5–7 are not published. Formula 1 prices the US Grand Prix and the 2026 Drivers' Championship from official points shares.

8. Confidence & Featured Rules

HIGH: ≥72.0% pick probability. MEDIUM: 60.0%–71.99% (or prior-season cap). LOW: <60.0%. Unlined: any missing primary input. A featured selection is the strongest market per match with probability ≥70.0% and fair American price inside -250..+10000; strong favourites are repriced into the band with alternate handicaps (including Asian quarter lines on normal-margin sports) drawn from the same model distribution, never by padding with ultra-short moneylines. Every featured selection carries a unique ≥30-word human write-up.

Every value published on /verified/ is traced to an official governing-body or league endpoint captured on 2026-10-22. Unconfirmed fields are left strictly blank (—) and listed on Flagged for Review.
Excluded per brief: Soccer/Football, Horse Racing, American Football, Boxing, UFC/MMA, Hurling, Cycling, Specials.
