How to read this

Prices are ours, not the market's

OLBG publishes tip percentages but no prices, and no bookmaker odds were used. Every price above is 1 / (probability × 1.04), i.e. our own fair price plus a 4% theoretical margin. They are not available prices and should not be read as such. The full formulas live in Methodology.

What "confidence" means here

Confidence grades are model classifications based on recorded sample size and model probability; they are not probabilities that have been independently validated, nor a guarantee of outcome. Sport-specific thresholds are described in Methodology. Evidence gaps are listed in Evidence status.
