Rules, maths and limits

Method

The desk is deterministic: captures in, pages out. Re-running the three build steps reproduces every number below. Prices are fair probabilities, not bookmaker quotes, and no margin is added anywhere.

Validation performed by the build

  • Each written prediction must name a selection that exists as an originated market on an upcoming fixture.
  • Each prediction must be at least 30 words; the current minimum is 84.
  • The words "model" and "verified" are rejected anywhere in the published prose, as the brief requires.
  • No two predictions share a four-word opening, and no sentence of eight words or more is repeated.
  • No prediction is attached to a fixture without a price.
  • Probability ceilings: every published probability is clamped to 5–95% because a single fixture always retains irreducible variance.

Known limits

  • Season-to-date inputs are three to eight games old for several competitions; they are labelled with their capture time on every row.
  • Volleyball and Rugby Union prices rest on very thin inputs (one same-pairing result and a last-three form string respectively) and are labelled medium or low confidence accordingly.
  • The golf and motor-racing fields are normalised over the players captured, with an explicit residual for the rest; these are the crudest numbers on the desk.
  • No price here accounts for team news, weather or travel. Those inputs were not readable.
