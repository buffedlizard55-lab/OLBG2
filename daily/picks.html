Why the confidence and the price window disagree

Four of the eight priced fixtures produce a winner above the 70% confidence bar, and one more sits just above it. All five of those prices are shorter than −250, so they fall outside the brief's price window even though they pass its confidence bar. The reverse is also true: the only prices long enough to sit in the window are alternate lines whose probability is close to 50%, and those are not confident picks. Publishing a quarter-game handicap purely to manufacture a −250 price would be inventing a market, so it was not done. The full reasoning, with the arithmetic, is on DL-05.
