CLOCK-QUIRKAllOLBG's rendered clock runs seven minutes ahead of the official UTC conversion
NHL game 2026020182 starts 2026-10-25T23:00:00Z, which is 19:00 US Eastern, and OLBG printed 'Tomorrow 19:07'. The same +7 minute offset appears on all twenty NHL rows checked. No OLBG clock string is used as a start time; the official feed is used where one was read.

HANDICAP-DEFAULTHandballNine handball rows show 'Tie' as the most-tipped selection
On the handball hub the modal selection is 'Tie' on nine rows, several at 100% of a small sample (7/7, 4/4, 3/3). A drawn handball match is uncommon, so a 'Tie' row carrying every tip is more likely a rendering of the draw leg of a three-way market than a genuine crowd position. Those rows are published as listed but are not used to originate a price.

The ATP scores page returned only page chrome and no draw, and the WTA draw pages were not read. Fixture, players and OLBG's clock are recorded; the official start time and the tournament name are left blank on every tennis row rather than guessed.

TIER-B-PRICINGAllThirteen sports carry no governing-body statistics in this capture
Only the five NHL fixtures have league totals read from an official feed. For every other fixture the win price is a normalised read of the tip share OLBG itself publishes, and only the win market is originated, because a handicap or a total cannot be derived without scoring data. Those rows are graded no higher than medium however short the price.
