Method

How a fixture becomes a priced selection.

Six steps, applied identically to every fixture. The chain never starts from a bookmaker price.

1. Discovery and listing cross-check

The fifteen sports are taken from the brief. OLBG's own sport routes are recorded so the mapping is auditable: Ice Hockey is OLBG id 13, Gaelic Football is 25 and Hurling is 26, and id 11 is American Football. Every one of the fifteen hubs was read on 2026-10-24. OLBG is a listing source only.

2. Fixture confirmation

A fixture is published only when a governing body, league or tour page names both participants and a start time or a scheduled round. The link on the row is that page. Where only one side is official (for example the USA Eagles, whose squad was not published), the row is published with a stated blank.

3. Inputs

Only official numbers enter the pricing: records, goals or points for and against, games played, league tables, match reports, published averages, semi-final times, scorelines and probable pitchers. Where a number could not be read, the fixture is blank rather than filled.

4. Line origination

Sport familyTransform
Ice hockeyShrunk multiplicative Poisson over a 0–16 goal grid; overtime and shootout split from the tie mass; puck line from P(margin ≥ 2)
BaseballRun expectancy from team runs per game blended with the announced starters' ERA and WHIP; extra-inning tie mass split 52/48
BasketballWin-rate logit with a three-win pseudo-count prior and a 0.22 home-court term, plus 1.6 points of margin per confirmed absence; normal margin and total
Rugby unionLeague-table attack and defence with shrinkage on games played, or points difference per game where that is all the league publishes; normal margin and total
Rugby leagueRound 1 winning margins damped into an expected points line (no per-team points table is published in the World Cup match centre)
CricketSeries win-loss differential into a logit; totals held at 50/50 where no venue par exists
Darts, tennis, volleyballRace-to-target state model, so every set or leg score is enumerated and the handicap and total fall out of the same grid
Golf, F1, greyhoundsOutright fields priced from the leaderboard, the practice order and the semi-final clock respectively

5. Confidence

A selection is high when a two-way price is at least 60% at a price inside −250…+10000, and the sport's inputs are complete. Outright fields are graded on their own scale, because a 30-runner field never produces a 60% favourite. Each sport then has a ceiling; the ceiling and its reason are printed on the sport page and in data/meridian/picks.json.

6. Prices

Every published price is the reciprocal of the probability: decimal = 1/p, and the American figure follows from that same rounded decimal, so the two always agree. There is no margin and no bookmaker input. A price of −250 means we think the selection wins one time in 1.40.

What would make this desk wrong

Small samples, missing injury sheets, a client-rendered table, an unpublished average, or a fixture whose start time was captured from a page that later changed. Each of those is named on the Flagged page rather than buried.
