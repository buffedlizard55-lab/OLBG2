Sport-level disclosures

  • Gaelic football publishes three fixtures with no price at all. Munster GAA and the county boards publish fixtures, venues and throw-in times but no rating or points table for club sides, so any line would be invented.
  • NBA prices use preseason net ratings from NBA.com's official Week 1 power rankings, not regular-season form; the four rows are capped and flagged.
  • Rugby league uses round-one score differentials as a strength input because no rating table exists after one round.
  • Golf, Formula 1 and greyhounds publish outright markets only, priced from an official points or prize-money table; the field beyond the named contenders is not modelled.
  • Darts uses the official European Tour seed bracket; unseeded players are treated as seed 40, which is an assumption and is stated on every affected row.
