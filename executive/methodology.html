Methodology & evidence limits

Coverage and freshness

Sports in scope: Ice Hockey, Basketball, Motor Racing, Tennis, Golf, Cricket, Darts, Snooker, Volleyball, Rugby Union, Handball, Gaelic Sports, Greyhounds, Baseball and Rugby League — the exact 15 categories requested. Fixture pairings and numeric facts are captured live from official governing-body sources on 24 October 2026 and are not continuously refreshed.

Fixture confirmation

Every priced row carries the official source URL it was read from. Where a governing body publishes no comparable numeric table the value is left blank and flagged rather than filled from another sport's convention.

Our own lines

Each fixture carries two per-side strength inputs taken from the linked official source (NHL points percentage, NBA power-ranking slot, ATP/WST/PDC ranking, SuperLega round points, F1 championship points, OWGR position, and so on). Two-way probabilities are computed as p(home) = sigmoid(K·(s_home − s_away) + home_edge) with K = 3.0 and home_edge = 0.15; outright probabilities are the event-position estimates recorded in the fixture. Fair American and decimal prices are the reciprocals of those probabilities. No margin is added and no OLBG quote is used.

Confidence

Conviction is high at probability ≥ 0.70, medium at ≥ 0.60, low below 0.60. A fixture whose only strength input is a league-average default is forced to low, so an unmodelled matchup can never masquerade as a conviction call.

The −250 to +10000 window

The requested band is tested against our fair American price. High-conviction picks in the window sit at probability 0.70–0.714; picks priced below −250 are stronger favourites and are shown separately, not dropped.

Previews

Each preview is written uniquely from the captured facts. Where a sport has fewer than five priced fixtures on this date, the shortfall is flagged rather than padded with invented selections.
