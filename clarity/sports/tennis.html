Basilashvili +1.5 sets covers a final where Medvedev's edge is one break

medium · Sun 25 Oct 10:00 UTC

Nikoloz Basilashvili +1.5 · Set handicap · -119

Back Nikoloz Basilashvili +1.5 on the set handicap, which pays if he wins a set or loses in three. Medvedev is the top seed with a Tour-leading 37 hard-court wins in 2026 and a 10-1 record at the Almaty Open, and he led Alexander Bublik 6-3 when the Kazakh retired in the semi-final. Basilashvili qualified, then beat Adrian Mannarino 6-3 6-1, is guaranteed a return to the Top 100 and is chasing a sixth tour title.
