L07model limitationWorld Series model carries no home-advantage term, so all three games price identically

No official MLB home/away split was captured this session, so the Poisson model applies no home edge. Games 1, 2 and 3 therefore return the same match-winner probabilities (Milwaukee 59.4%) even though game 3 is played in St. Petersburg. The prices are internally consistent with the inputs but understate the home side in games 1-2.

Action: Disclosed. Confidence for all three is medium, never high, which is consistent with the uncertainty.

https://statsapi.mlb.com/api/v1/standings?leagueId=103,104&season=2026&standin...
L08model limitationRugby spreads are estimated from eight matches

The margin spread (18.96 points) and total spread (12.93 points) used for URC handicap and total prices are the sample standard deviations of the eight official Round 3 results. Eight matches is a small sample, so handicap and total prices are wider than a full-season estimate would give.

Action: Disclosed in every row's model block. High-confidence picks are taken from the match-winner market only.

https://stats.unitedrugby.com/match-centre/2026-27/5
L09disclosureURC kick-off times are local, not UTC

The URC match centre states 'All times shown are Local'. Kick-off times are stored exactly as published and are not converted, so rows for South African, Italian and British venues are not directly comparable.

Action: Each row labels its time as local.
