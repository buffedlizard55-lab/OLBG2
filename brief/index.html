Executive summary — the strongest picks on the board

How the two rules interact. A selection needs a probability of at least 70% to be called high confidence, and a fair price between −250 and +10000 to sit inside the requested window. A 70% chance converts to about −233, so the two conditions only overlap in the thin −250 to −233 slice. We publish the tier plainly rather than bending either rule: Tier A is high confidence and inside the window; Tier B is high confidence but priced shorter than −250; Tier C is inside the window but below 70%.

What this desk does not claim

  • It is not a complete OLBG slate. OLBG's public tips pages render their event tables client-side and return "No tips found" to a non-browser client; the evidence is on the OLBG page. OLBG's taxonomy, sport IDs and market vocabulary are reproduced there, and the fixtures themselves come from each sport's governing body.
  • It is not a live feed. Every row carries the capture time of the page it came from. Rows whose start time had passed by 19:45Z are labelled as such and excluded from selections.
  • It does not invent prices for matches with no official input. Four sports have fixtures but no line today: tennis (no pairing published), cricket, Gaelic sports and greyhounds. Darts has fixtures but no ranking source. Each is named with its reason.
  • No prediction asserts an injury, a suspension or a team-news item. No official injury feed could be read in this window, so those claims are absent rather than guessed.
