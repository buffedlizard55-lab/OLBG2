Why there are no high-confidence predictions

A model probability of 70% is not evidence that a model is calibrated. High confidence requires p ≥ 70%, exact fair decimal 1.40–101.00 (−250…+10000 American), and independent calibration. The latter is not established here. Stronger probabilities alone do not override that safeguard. No high write-ups were fabricated.

Winner estimates split a regulation tie 50/50 as an explicit assumption. Handicap and total estimates are regulation-only; they are not quotes for a bookmaker’s overtime-inclusive market. Full regulation three-way probabilities are in the machine-readable output.
