What this checked

Every committed fixture row was scanned for an HTTPS publisher link and source ownership; every featured prediction was scanned for a 30-word minimum and a classified publisher link. Specific PDC, PGA Tour and Riot Games claims were then compared with their official publications. An official-looking hostname is not proof that every claim on that row is correct.
