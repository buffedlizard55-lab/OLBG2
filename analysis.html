3. The chance distribution, and why the band is so narrow

A high grade requires our own probability to clear 70%. The brief's band requires the price to be no shorter than −250, which is 40.0% implied. Both conditions together leave only the sliver between 70.0% and 71.4%, so the honest answer is that a high-confidence pick is almost never a natural price — it is an alternate line quoted up to the ceiling.

5. Time, which is where most of the mess is

17 matched pairs carry a start time on both sides. 16 agree to within fifteen minutes and 1 do not: nba-0022600099 (230 min, OLBG: CHI Bulls @ MIL Bucks). The desk shows both values and flags the disagreement instead of choosing. A further 78 of the 122 graded selections have no clock time at all in the captured layer, so they cannot be placed on a day; the daily summary therefore covers 44 selections, not 122.

6. What would have to change to close the gaps

  • 86 more priced fixtures would need a captured strength input for each competition in table 1 — a league table, a form line or a published rating. The desk will not price a match from the name of the teams.
  • 42 more predictions to reach five per sport would need confident selections, not just fixtures: golf, the greyhounds and Gaelic football currently have no selection above low confidence, so a write-up there would be a fabrication with a source link attached.
  • A reproducible fixture layer. data/cadence/captures/ stores readings, not response bodies, so this session cannot re-derive the facts it publishes — the single biggest weakness in the evidence chain, recorded as U-10.

What this desk is. A dated, source-linked research record of sport listed on OLBG, of our own originated prices, and of confidence-graded selections. It is not a live odds feed and it does not place bets.

Every price, probability and confidence label is our own estimate, derived from the official statistics named on the row. Values that could not be confirmed are left blank and listed on Flagged for review. Capture times and source files are printed on every table.
