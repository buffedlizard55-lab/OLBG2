Methodology, markets and the high-confidence gate

Every price on this layer is originated from official statistics captured on 22 October 2026. Nothing is copied from a bookmaker and nothing is inherited from another layer of this repository.

The high-confidence gate

high = model probability ≥ 0.7000 and a fair no-vig American price inside -250 … +10000. A fair price of -250 corresponds to p = 0.714286, so the band is a 1.43-point-wide window. medium = probability ≥ 0.60, and also any probability above 0.70 whose fair price is shorter than -250, because ultra-short chalk is capped. low = everything else.

The gate is applied to every originated market on both sides, not only to the two-way moneyline. That matters: a moneyline can only satisfy the window between 70.0% and 71.43%, which almost never happens, whereas an alternate handicap or total line can be chosen so a genuinely strong opinion lands inside the band. Of 29 high-confidence picks, 28 come from an alternate handicap or total line and 1 from a moneyline.

Pricing convention

Prices are fair and carry no vigourish: fair_decimal = 1 / probability rounded to two decimals, converted to American as +100 × (decimal - 1) above 2.00 and -100 / (decimal - 1) below it. Handicap thresholds are always half-points so no line can push, and the home side is posted at minus the threshold while the away side is posted at plus it, making the two sides exact complements.

Assumptions that are ours, not the sport's (flag CU-23)

No official source publishes a home advantage, a shrinkage prior or a standard deviation. These are chosen by this layer and are listed in full so the arithmetic can be re-run with different values:

  • Home advantage: NHL ×1.05 multiplicative on goals; EuroLeague +3.0 points; URC +3.0 points; EHF +0.4 goals; MLB +0.25 runs. Snooker and motor racing have no home advantage.
  • Shrinkage priors (w = games / (games + prior)): NHL 8, EuroLeague 6, URC 6, EHF 3, MLB 20, snooker 6 matches. Ratings are 1 + w × (rate / league_average - 1), so a club with no games sits exactly at league average.
  • Dispersion: hockey, handball, baseball and snooker need none because the score grid is exact. EuroLeague margin sd 11.0 and total sd 15.0; URC margin sd 13.0 and total sd 16.0, with a draw requiring the margin inside ±1 point.
  • Alternate-line radii are capped per sport so a published line is one a book would plausibly post rather than an artefact of searching a huge grid.

Verification performed

  • Every fixture, record, standing, venue, time and result was returned by an official governing-body, league or tour endpoint during this session and stored verbatim under data/current/capture/.
  • The four grid and normal models were recomputed from the raw captures through an independent code path and agreed with the published probabilities to four decimal places.
  • Arithmetic in every captured table was re-checked: goals for minus goals against equals the published difference, and wins plus draws plus losses equals games played, for all 24 EHF clubs, all 30 MLB clubs, all 20 EuroLeague clubs and all 16 URC clubs.
  • Cross-validation: the EuroLeague standings positions 1 to 20 match the club codes printed beside each fixture in the official game centre; NHL records beside the 23 and 24 October fixtures match the 21 October team-summary snapshot for six clubs.
  • No bookmaker price, aggregator price or third-party statistic was read at any point.
