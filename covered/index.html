Executive summary

Coverage. Seven of the fifteen sports in the brief have source-confirmed fixtures on 23-25 October 2026: Ice Hockey, Basketball, Baseball, Motor Racing, Rugby League, Snooker and Gaelic Sports (the Gaelic row is flagged). Tennis has observed rows but no fixture list. Darts appears on OLBG only. Lines. We originated 14 win-match lines, all for NHL and NBA fixtures. Every line is labelled low confidence. The team inputs are a handful of games (NHL 9-10 per team, NBA one game per team), so the lines are weak by design.

High-confidence picks. None. The highest originated probability in this snapshot is Indiana Pacers at 59.0% (Basketball, low). Under the brief's high-confidence rule (more than 70% and a fair price between -250 and +10000), no selection qualifies, so no written predictions are published.

What is blank and why. Handicap, total and other markets were not originated, because the official pages read did not carry the scoring or margin inputs needed. Those blanks are listed on each sport page. Nothing was filled in by inference.

Decision needed. The literal band (more than 70% and fair price between -250 and +10000) admits only about 70.0% to 71.4%. See Flagged for Review, item FL-01.
