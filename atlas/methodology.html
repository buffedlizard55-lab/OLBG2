Methodology

1. What gets published

A fixture is published only if this session opened the governing-body URL stored on the row and the fixture was there — same teams, same date, same venue. Everything else is either blank (values not confirmed) or on the flagged page (rows that contradict their source).

2. Confidence rule

  • high — model probability ≥ 70% and the match's own prices sit between -250 and +10000.
  • medium — model probability ≥ 60%, or ≥ 70% on a match priced shorter than -250.
  • low — under 60%.
Two defensible readings, both shown. The brief says to focus high-confidence picks on “matches that sit between -250 and +10000”. This desk reads that as a filter on the match, so a selection at 87% on the puck line is graded high even though its own price (-690) is shorter than -250. Earlier layers in this repository read it as a filter on the selection, which is stricter. Both counts are printed: 41 written predictions, of which 6 are also priced inside the window themselves and are marked featured on the predictions page.

3. NHL model

w        = gp / (gp + 8)
attack_i  = 1 + w * ((gf_i/gp_i) / L - 1)
defence_i = 1 + w * ((ga_i/gp_i) / L - 1)
L         = league goals per team-game, from the same table
lambda_home = L * attack_home * defence_away * 1.044
lambda_away = L * attack_away * defence_home / 1.044
moneyline   = Poisson grid to 15 goals, tied games split 50/50
puck line   = 1.5 goals, P(home covers) = P(home margin ≥ 2)
total       = the half-goal line whose P(over) is closest to 0.50

Inputs are official. Every term comes from nhl.com/standings as published on 22 October 2026. The constants 8 (shrinkage games) and 1.044 (home-ice multiplier, about a 9% goal ratio) are our own origination, not measured values.

4. MLB model

pythag_i  = RS^1.83 / (RS^1.83 + RA^1.83)
p         = log5(pythag_home, pythag_away), shifted by a logit home edge of 0.16
runs      = Poisson rates from the two clubs' official runs scored/allowed per game
run line  = 1.5 runs from the Skellam grid on those two rates
total     = the half-run line whose P(over) is closest to 0.50

Inputs: the official MLB Stats API standings endpoint for the completed 2026 regular season.

5. What is deliberately blank

Where no official strength input could be read, the row is published with the fixture and its link but with every price blank. That applies to all 74 NBA rows (stats.nba.com unreachable, and the new season is one game old), the 9 EuroLeague rows (the official standings endpoint returned an empty payload), the 10 tennis rows (the ATP rankings table could not be read) and the 143 rows inherited from the earlier desk that this session did not re-open.

6. Prices

Every price on this site is a fair price originated by this project: 1 ÷ our model probability, with no margin and no bookmaker input. No bookmaker price is read, scraped or compared anywhere in this repository.
