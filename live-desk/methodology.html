Method

How a match gets on this desk, how a price is originated, and what the desk does not claim.

1. The fixture list is OLBG's

The desk reads the sixteen OLBG sport hub pages that cover the fifteen requested sports (Gaelic Football and Hurling are the two OLBG pages behind Gaelic Sports) and keeps every upcoming event with a quoted selection. An event is dropped only if OLBG itself marks it expired. Nothing is added from a governing body that OLBG did not list.

2. Verification

For ice hockey, baseball, basketball, tennis and motor racing the desk also reads the official feed and matches the participants. Those rows are marked official. Every other row keeps its OLBG link, is marked OLBG only, and is listed on the review page. The official pages that were consulted are named on the sources page with the size of the text read from each.

3. Our price, not OLBG's

OLBG quotes one price for the selection it tips. That number is shown as OLBG quote and is never reused as our line. Our number is produced as follows.

Where none of those inputs exists, the desk falls back to OLBG's published tip share, shrunk halfway to an even split, and labels the row crowd-consensus. Where even that is absent the fixture is published with no price at all.

4. Confidence and the price window

High requires a probability of at least 70% and an official numeric input. Medium is 60–70% (or any consensus row at 60%+). Everything else is low. The price window is shown separately: a fair price inside −250 to +10000 is marked in window. A 75% favourite is priced near −300, which is outside the window while still being a high-confidence pick — both facts are printed rather than one replacing the other.

5. What this desk does not claim

  • It is not a bookmaker feed and it does not place bets.
  • It does not claim to hold every market OLBG offers; it prices the selection OLBG publishes for each event.
  • It does not claim injuries, team news or line-ups unless an official page in the sources table carried them.
  • It does not convert venue-local tip times to UTC; where the league serves local time, the row says so.
Every row links to the page it was read from. Nothing on this site is a bookmaker quote presented as our own number, and no value is filled in when the source did not carry it.
