Every graded selection

One row per fixture. The selection is the OLBG-listed market we price; the price is ours, from the basis named. Quoted OLBG prices are shown beside it, and the At that price column says whether the selection is worth backing: it reads back only when our price is shorter than the quote, and no bet when the market is the shorter side.

Every row links to the page it was read from. Nothing on this site is a bookmaker quote presented as our own number, and no value is filled in when the source did not carry it.
