## Executive conclusion

The repository already contains a static dashboard, sport pages, a verification ledger, sourced prediction cards, and a flagged-items page. The local build/test gates pass. However, the published rows do **not** yet demonstrate a complete, live OLBG match inventory, and the odds-window picks are model-implied prices rather than bookmaker market prices. Several metadata and narrative requirements are also not met or are presented inconsistently. These gaps are recorded here rather than silently filled.
