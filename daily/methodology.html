Methodology

Verification chain

  1. Discovery: OLBG sport hubs (/betting-tips/{Sport}/{sport_id}, re-fetched 2026-10-22) define the 17-sport eligible taxonomy after exclusions.
  2. Confirmation: fixtures come only from official league/governing-body captures stored in this repo (NHL score API, NBA.com, EuroLeague, LNR, URC, EHF, PDC, MLB Stats API, formula1.com), with fresh 2026-10-22 confirmation fetches recorded in research/daily-b499dfe1.md.
  3. Origination: our own models (below). No bookmaker/OLBG price is read.
  4. Publication: every row links its official source; unverifiable values stay blank and flagged.

Models

  • NHL / MLB / EHF — Poisson attack-defence: shrunk scoring & concession rates vs league average (NHL prior 8 GP, home +5%; EHF prior 3, home +0.4 goals; MLB 162-game rates, home +2%), full score grid → winner (ties split for OT/SO; EHF also 1X2), ±1.5 handicap/run/puck line (+ one alternate step), totals around expected total.
  • EuroLeague / Top 14 / Pro D2 / URC — margin-normal: expected scores from shrunk PF/PA attack-defence, σ 11 (EuroLeague) / 14 (rugby), home +3 pts → winner, spread, totals, with alternate lines stepped to search the −250…+10000 band at 1-point increments (wider steps beyond ±6).
  • NBA — prior-season logit: 2025-26 records shrunk 25% to .500, rating diff ×11 + 0.20 home logit → moneyline & spread. Totals not originated (no verified PF/PA). Confidence capped at medium (0 current-season games at capture).
  • Darts — Bradley-Terry on PDC Werner Rankings Ladder prize money: damped log-prize per-leg probability → first-to-6 match, ±1.5 legs, O/U 9.5 legs (negative-binomial race).
  • F1 outright — points-share blend: 65% championship-points share + 35% recorded race-wins share, normalised across 23 drivers. Capped at medium.

Confidence & the focus band

High requires model p ≥ 70% and fair American price (from rounded no-vig decimal 1/p) inside −250…+10000 and the sample gate (NHL ≥7 GP both, EHF ≥4, rugby ≥3 rounds; NBA/MLB/F1 capped for stated reasons). Medium ≥60%, low below. Alternate handicap/total lines are used to express 70%+ opinions inside the band; the in-band selection closest to the 70% floor is chosen. If no in-band selection exists, the primary pick is the plain match winner — never an extreme alternate at ~1.01.

Top 5 & predictions

Per sport, matches are ranked by primary-pick probability; the top five (or fewer, blank-flagged) form the daily five. A written prediction (≥30 words, unique) accompanies every Top-5 pick and every high-confidence pick, built only from seeded official facts: records, per-game scoring/concession, form lines, rankings/prize money, and official player leaders (NHL feed). No injuries, lineups, transfers or travel are asserted.

Limitations

  • Early-season samples (NHL 7–12 GP, URC 3 rounds) are noisy; shrinkage guards but does not fix roster change.
  • NBA priors are a season old. MLB has no probable-pitcher input. F1 has no qualifying input.
  • Coverage is the captured official competitions only — not every event worldwide. Gaps are enumerated on the Flagged page.
  • Fair prices carry no margin; they are not value claims against any market.
