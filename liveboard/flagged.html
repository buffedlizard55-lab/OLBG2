mediumF-11 · OLBG's time strings mix two time zones

All

North American events display US Eastern: MLB's World Series opener reads 'Today 20:00' against an official first pitch of 2026-10-24T00:00:00Z, i.e. 20:00 EDT. European events display UK local: URC's own match centre gives 14:45 local for Cardiff v Sharks and OLBG shows 14:45. The strings are stored exactly as displayed and are never silently converted, so a reader should not treat them as UTC.

Action: Every table shows the original display string and says so in the column header. Official UTC times are quoted only where a governing body published them.

Sources: stats.unitedrugby.com/match-centre/2026-27/4 · statsapi.mlb.com/api/v1/teams/158?hydrate=standings

highF-14 · The two published gates are nearly mutually exclusive

All

-250 is an implied 71.4%. Requiring at least 70% confidence AND a price no shorter than -250 therefore describes a window of roughly one and a half percentage points. On this board ten selections clear 70% and exactly two of them (Aurillac at 71.4%, New South Wales at 71.4%) sit inside the band; the rest are too short. Five per sport inside that window is not attainable from these fixtures without dropping one of the two gates.

Action: Both gate results are published separately so the reader can see which gate excludes what. The band was not widened.

Sources: liveboard/methodology.html
