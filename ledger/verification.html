How the check was performed

  1. Every OLBG sport hub was fetched and parsed for its embedded tip objects: event name, start time, market, quoted price and OLBG's own confidence figure. That gives the slate, and it is the only thing OLBG is used for.
  2. Official schedule feeds were fetched for the sports that publish one (NHL, MLB, EuroLeague) and parsed into fixtures with fixture ids, venues and UTC start times.
  3. Each OLBG event was compared with those feeds by participant name and a 36-hour time window. A hit is an official fixture and links its game page.
  4. For sports without a feed, the text of each official competition page was normalised and searched for the two participants on opposite sides of the same versus marker. A hit is an official page and the surrounding text is stored on the row as evidence.
  5. Everything else is unmatched. It keeps its OLBG listing and appears on the flagged page.
