OLBG: what the site actually serves

Read first-hand from olbg.com during the capture window across the sixteen sportIds that cover the fifteen mandated sports. OLBG is used for discovery and taxonomy only: it is not the source of a single fixture fact or price on this desk.

Sport index

Hub route: /betting-tips/{Sport}/{id}. Event route: /betting-tips/{Sport}/{Group}/{Competition}/{Event_Name}/{id}?event_id={eventId}. The sport name is case-sensitive in the path. Why this matters for the brief. OLBG's percentage is the share of community tips on a selection, not a probability and not a price. Using it as either would manufacture an edge that does not exist. The desk records the tip split only as a description of OLBG, never as an input.
Every published row links to the official governing-body page it came from. Values that an official source did not publish are left blank and listed on Flagged for review. Prices are the desk's own fair lines, never observed bookmaker quotes unless a row says so.
