Cleanroom Mathematical Origination & Verification Rules

Origination Models, Market Coverage & Confidence Grading

All fair probabilities and decimal prices (1 / p, zero bookmaker overround) are computed deterministically in scripts/verified_model.py using only the Python standard library and verified primary inputs from scripts/verified_seed.py and data/inputs/*.json.

1. Ice Hockey (NHL — 16 Games)

Empirical-Bayes shrunk attack/defence goal rates (k = 8 games shrinkage toward league mean goals/game) feeding a 0–25 bivariate Poisson grid with 52%/48% home/away OT/SO split. Originates 2-way Moneyline, -1.5/+1.5 Puck Line, and 5.5/6.5 Total Goals.

2. Basketball (NBA, EuroLeague, EuroCup — 25 Games)

NBA uses shrunk 2025-26 win-rate logit prior (capped at MEDIUM; totals left blank because nba.com/standings omits PF/PA). EuroLeague (10 R7 games) & BKT EuroCup (4 R4 games) use shrunk net efficiency margin normal models across Moneyline, Spread, and Over/Under Total Points.

3. Rugby Union (Top 14, Pro D2, URC — 23 Matches)

Normal scoring-margin & total-points distribution with empirical-Bayes shrinkage (k = 4 matches) and home advantage (+4.5 pts) across 1X2 Match Result, Half-Point Handicap, and Total Points.

4. Handball (EHF Champions League — 12 Matches)

Full-support 0–60 bivariate Poisson model trained on official 4-match Group Phase GF/GA tables (k = 4), originating 1X2 Match Winner, Half-Goal Handicap, and Over/Under Total Goals.

5. Darts (PDC European Championship — 16 Ties)

Exact negative-binomial / binomial race-to-6-legs Markov calculation from log two-year official PDC Werner Ranking Ladder prize sums for all 32 players, pricing Match Winner, -1.5/+1.5 Leg Handicap, and Over/Under 9.5 Total Legs.

6. Tennis & Snooker (7 ATP + 2 WST Matches)

Exact best-of-3 sets (ATP Top-100 points) and best-of-7 frames (WST Top-36 prize money) combinatorial models pricing Match Winner, -1.5/+1.5 Set/Frame Handicap, and Over/Under Total Sets/Frames. Matches involving unconfirmed ranks outside SSR pages are left blank.

7. Baseball & Motor Racing (MLB WS G1 + 2 F1 Markets)

MLB World Series Game 1 (TB 98-64, 736 RS / 650 RA at MIL 103-59, 832 RS / 618 RA) uses shrunk run-rate Poisson across Moneyline, Run Line, and Total Runs. FIA Formula 1 prices Round 18 & 2026 Drivers' Championship from official 17-round points shares.

8. Confidence Thresholds

HIGH: ≥72.0% model probability backed by current-season official stats (requires bespoke ≥30-word human editorial preview). MEDIUM: 60.0%–71.99% (or prior-season cap). LOW: <60.0%. Unlined: Any missing primary input.

Every value published on /verified/ is traced to an official governing-body or league endpoint captured on 2026-10-21. Unconfirmed fields are left strictly blank (—) and listed on Flagged for Review.
Excluded per brief: Soccer/Football, Horse Racing, American Football, Boxing, UFC/MMA, Hurling, Cycling, Specials.
