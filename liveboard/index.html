What is solid

  • Every fixture row links the OLBG page it was read from.
  • Every official number links the governing body endpoint that returned it.
  • Every price is reproducible: scripts/liveboard_model.py rebuilds the file byte for byte.
  • Every prediction states a selection in plain words, with its facts linked underneath.

What is not

  • Five written predictions per sport was not reached. Seven were written, in three sports.
  • No official injury list was obtained, so no prediction claims one.
  • OLBG shows no odds to a logged-out visitor, so its tip share is the only market signal available.
  • OLBG's “Load More” pagination was not followed; these lists are first-page slices.

Research aid, not betting advice. Prices here are originated by this project, not quotes from a bookmaker.
