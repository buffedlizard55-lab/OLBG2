How a price is made

Each player gets a rating from their official ATP seeding plus verified sets won and lost in the tournament. The rating gap becomes a win probability through a logistic curve; the gap in games becomes a normal margin distribution, which prices every alternate handicap and total on the lines page. The constants are ours, listed in full on the methodology page, and reproducible with python3 scripts/daily_audit_model.py. No bookmaker price is read, compared or implied anywhere in this layer.

Research snapshot, not a live feed and not betting advice. Prices are this layer's own fair odds derived from the model on the methodology page; they are not bookmaker quotes. A linked official page records provenance for the fixture and the inputs on that row — it is not proof that every value beside it is current. Values that could not be confirmed are blank and listed on Flagged for Review. 18+.
