Coverage and evidence

  • 93 of 119 rows (78%) carry originated prices — moneyline and handicap on every lined row, plus totals wherever the official feed publishes scoring data; 26 rows are blank with a stated reason.
  • 85 of 119 rows cite a URL that identifies the individual fixture; the remaining 34 cite a competition or rankings page, disclosed as VF-11.
  • 6 official source families were re-read line by line on 2026-10-22 and every compared figure matched except one corrected form sentence; the coverage limit, and which rows were not re-read, are published as VF-23. The snapshot itself was captured on 2026-10-22.
  • The snapshot spans 39 competition labels in 13 sports; Aussie Rules and eSports returned no active fixtures and are shown empty rather than skipped.

Prices and confidence

  • Confidence split: 18 high, 26 medium, 49 low, 26 unlined/no pick.
  • 14 band picks sit at fair odds 1.40-1.42 with model probabilities of 70.0-71.4%, the only window satisfying both the ≥70% rule and the −250 floor.
  • Moneyline picks graded high (≥72%) whose fair price is shorter than the −250 floor stay on the sport tables without a written prediction, rather than padding the picks page with ultra-short prices.
  • The longest fair price anywhere in the 14 predictions is 1.42, inside the +10000 ceiling.

What this project does — and does not do

Does

  • Records OLBG's sport taxonomy and excludes the eight categories in the brief.
  • Reads fixture, date, venue and form data from governing bodies or leagues.
  • Originates its own moneyline, handicap and total prices from those inputs.
  • Writes unique, sourced predictions for every published high-confidence pick.
  • Leaves unconfirmed values blank and publishes them for review.

Does not

  • Claim a live, exhaustive OLBG schedule — the capture is dated 2026-10-22 01:30 UTC.
  • Copy bookmaker odds or tipster percentages into the model.
  • Fill a missing venue, ranking or total with an inferred value.
  • Guarantee any selection; confidence is an estimate, not certainty.
  • Present past fixtures as upcoming.
