How lines are originated

Rugby union. Expected points are built from the official table: a club's points for per game and its opponent's points against per game, each scaled by the table's average scoring rate. The home side is then shifted by half the home advantage measured in the official Round 3 results (+4.81 points to the home side, -4.81 to the away side, from a mean home margin of +9.63 over 8 matches), so the expected total is not inflated. Match-winner prices use a normal model on the margin with the sample spread of those results (18.96 points); the draw price is the season draw rate read from the table (3 drawn matches in 24 played, 12.5%). Handicap and total prices use the same margin spread and a total spread of 12.93 points.

Baseball. Independent Poisson model on runs. Each side's run rate comes from the official standings (runs scored and allowed over 162 games) and is rescaled by the two finalists' own average run environment, so no league constant that was not captured is assumed. Tied scorelines are split 50/50 as extra innings. No home advantage is applied because no official home/away split was captured (flag L07).

Pricing. Fair prices are divided by 1.04, a disclosed 4% overround, then converted to American odds. Confidence. high = model probability ≥ 0.70 with a fair no-vig American price inside [-250, +10000] (rule applied 2026-10-22); medium = 0.55-0.70 or above-70% chalk outside the window; low < 0.55, on the model probability of the match-winner selection.

Rules this layer follows

A value is published only when an official source returned it in this session's capture. Anything unreachable stays blank and appears on the flagged page. No value is inferred from memory, and no secondary outlet is used as a factual source.

Prices are originated by this project's own model, not copied from any bookmaker. Unverified values are left blank and listed on the flagged page. Nothing is inferred to fill a gap.
