Method

Short enough to check line by line. The script that produces every number is scripts/liveboard_model.py; the script that produces this site is scripts/liveboard_build.py. Both read only the two JSON files in data/liveboard/.

1. Fixtures come from OLBG

Each sport hub is /betting-tips/{Sport}/{id}. The hub lists the event, the market OLBG is showing for it, the most-tipped selection and the tip split. Those five fields are copied verbatim, including OLBG's own time string. No timezone conversion is applied because OLBG's display is not a UTC source.

2. Probability comes from one of two places

Official inputs. Baseball uses Bill James' log5 on the two clubs' regular-season winning percentages with a home term; run lines and totals come from a normal approximation on season runs scored and allowed. The two NHL games use the mean implied probability of the moneylines the NHL's own feed publishes, because that endpoint carries prices but not club records. EuroLeague uses point differential per game. Snooker uses the log of World Snooker Tour ranking money. Formula 1 uses championship points share, discounted for single-race variance.

OLBG tip share. Everywhere else the probability is the Laplace-smoothed tip split, (tips + 1) / (total + 2). The smoothing is what stops a 1-of-1 tip becoming a 100% chance. These rows are labelled on every table; they are our own transformation of OLBG's sentiment, not a bookmaker price.

3. Prices

Fair decimal is 1 ÷ probability. Our offered price divides that by (1 − margin), with the margin listed per sport in the script. American odds are derived from the decimal in the usual way.

4. Labels

  • high — probability at or above 70%, and at least six tips behind it when the price is tip-share based.
  • medium — probability at or above 60%.
  • low — everything else.
  • in band — our offered American price sits between −250 and +10000, as the brief specifies.

5. What the band really does

A fair 70% shot is about −233, and a fair 75% shot is about −300 once a five per cent margin is applied. So “70% or better” and “−250 or longer” overlap in a window of roughly 70–75%. That is not a bug: it is the consequence of asking for both a high strike rate and a price that is not prohibitively short. Strong favourites such as Gloucester at 89% are correctly excluded from the band while still appearing in the per-sport top five.

6. Rules that were not bent

No value is inferred, filled forward or copied from a secondary site. A fixture without an official input and without a tip sample is left unlined. A claim without an official page is not written into a prediction.

Research aid, not betting advice. Prices here are originated by this project, not quotes from a bookmaker. OLBG supplies the fixture list and its published tip shares; governing bodies supply every other number. Where an official number was not obtained, the value is blank and the row appears on Flagged for review.
