### 1.3 OLBG Market Structure Mapped Into Independent Origination

On OLBG event cards (`/betting-tips/<Sport>/<Comp>/<Event>/<Cat>?event_id=<ID>`), tipsters post selections across four recurring market families:
1. **Primary Match Outcome (`Win Match` / `Moneyline` / `1X2` where draws are regulation outcomes)**: Two-way moneyline (`NHL`, `NBA`, `EuroLeague`, `MLB`, `Tennis`, `Darts`, `Snooker`, `Cricket ODI`, `AFLW`, `Esports`, `Golf H2H`, `F1 H2H`, `Greyhound H2H`) or three-way `1X2` (`Handball`, `Rugby Union`, `Rugby League`).
2. **Primary Handicap / Spread (`Puck Line -1.5/+1.5`, `Run Line -1.5/+1.5`, `Point Spread`, `Set Handicap -1.5/+1.5`, `Leg Handicap`, `Frame Handicap`)**: Priced directly from the same underlying latent margin/score distribution (`Poisson`, `Normal`, `Exact Best-of-N Markov/Binomial`).
3. **Match Total (`Over / Under` Goals, Runs, Points, Games, Legs, Frames)**: Derived analytically from team/player offensive and defensive pace/efficiency parameters with symmetric vig (`4.0%`–`5.0%` overround) plus fair no-vig decimals & American odds.
4. **Actionable High-Confidence Selection Band (`-250` to `+10000` American / `1.40` to `101.00` Decimal)**: Whenever a match favorite's raw moneyline is steeper than `-250` (e.g., `-420`), OLBG sharp tipsters pivot into **minus handicap (`-1.5` sets/pucks/runs, `-2.5` legs/frames, `-6.5` to `-14.5` points)** so the actionable price sits squarely inside `[-250, +10000]`. `/slate/` originates both markets side-by-side and restricts every featured pick on `/slate/picks.html` to selections priced strictly inside `[-250, +10000]`.
