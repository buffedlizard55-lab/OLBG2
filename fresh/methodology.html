What this layer does

scripts/fresh_audit.py reads data/verified/matches.json, predictions.json, sports.json, flagged.json and README.md read-only and re-checks every row: eligible-sport membership, duplicate ids/fixtures, HTTPS official + OLBG links, winner/handicap/total probability sums (3-way markets include the draw), fair-odds = 1/probability and American conversion (with rounding tolerances for 4dp probabilities / 2dp odds), the uniform confidence gate, prediction length/uniqueness/cross-references/fact sources, blanks, dates, and README claim drift. Results are written to data/fresh/audit.json; scripts/fresh_build.py renders these pages. Neither script edits upstream data.

Confidence gate

High = model probability ≥70% AND fair American price inside −250…+10000 (decimal 1.40–101.00). Since a ≥70% probability implies fair decimal ≤1.43, passing high picks cluster at 70.0–71.4%. Anything else is medium (≥60%) or low. Picks that fail the gate but say 'high' are flagged (FR-01), never silently relabelled here. Gate reference: official NHL fixture used in the sample re-read.

Lines originated (upstream, audited here)

Moneyline/win-match, handicap (puck/point/goal/frame/leg/set), and match totals were investigated for every sport; markets are blank where official inputs were missing (e.g. NBA totals, snooker frame format, greyhound trap ratings). Prices are fair model prices from official governing-body statistics, not bookmaker quotes and not OLBG tipster prices.

Verification limit

Sandbox HTTP to sports hosts is blocked; this session re-read a 4-page official sample via browser fetch (FR-08) and mechanically audited the rest. A passing mechanical check is not first-hand verification of a fixture's facts.

Every row links to its official source. Blank = unverified, never inferred. Prices are originated fair prices, not bookmaker quotes.
