Originated lines

Two provenance classes, never mixed silently. Official inputs means the probability was estimated from a number returned by a governing body. OLBG tip share means it was estimated from the tip split OLBG publishes, smoothed with a Laplace prior so a single tip can never read as certainty.

Fair price is 1 ÷ probability. Our offered price applies the sport's margin, documented on the method page. A price is “in band” when it sits between −250 and +10000.
