Methodology & evidence limits

Coverage and freshness

Sports in scope: Golf, Volleyball, Rugby Union, Cricket, Snooker, Darts, Baseball, Gaelic Sports, Greyhounds, Tennis, Motor Racing, Handball, Ice Hockey, Basketball and Rugby League. The source inventory is captured on 23 October 2026 and is not continuously refreshed.

Fixture confirmation

Fixture pairings and numeric facts remain tied to their official source URLs. A missing start time is displayed as unconfirmed; no local-time conversion is inferred. To avoid stale selections, lines, picks and written previews are omitted unless the official start is explicitly after the 2026-10-23 21:45 UTC cutoff. OLBG is used only to discover its event rows and market taxonomy.

Independent fair prices

For each supported market, the ledger publishes the captured same-day official-input probability, then independently recalculates a fair American price and fair decimal price as its reciprocal. The underlying probability calculation is retained from the existing Cadence official-input evidence bundle; this desk does not claim a newly invented or separately calibrated probability engine. No margin is added, no OLBG consensus or quote is used as our line, and no line is claimed to be currently offered by a bookmaker. Where the official capture has no comparable numeric inputs, the market/pick remains blank.

Confidence

Confidence labels are computed from the stored probability and evidence tier; thin samples may be capped at medium. They are not calibrated guarantees. “High” is not an endorsement of a wager.

Odds band

The requested −250 through +10000 band is checked against our fair American price. A fair price inside the band does not establish bookmaker availability. Because −250 implies about 71.43%, the overlap with a ≥70% high threshold is narrow.

Predictions

Preview paragraphs are unique, at least 30 words, and cite the official facts used. Injury claims appear only when an official match-specific report was captured. Five previews per sport is a target, not permission to invent.
