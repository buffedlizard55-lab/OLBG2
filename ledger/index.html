Executive summary — 23 October 2026

Today's desk covers the fifteen requested OLBG sports using only fixtures read first-hand today on official league and federation pages. Prices are our own fair prices, built from published goals, runs, points, rankings and results — never from OLBG tip percentages or bookmaker odds. Where an official page would not give up readable data, the fixture is still listed, the price cell stays empty and the reason appears on the Flagged for Review page.

The two headline rules, honestly reconciled. A fair price of −250 is a 71.43% chance, so ‘above 70%’ and ‘−250 or bigger’ can both be true only between 70.00% and 71.43%. Those rare cases are Tier A (4 today). Selections above 70% at shorter prices are Tier B; the strongest remaining in-band chances are Tier C. Every row carries its tier and an in-band marker.
