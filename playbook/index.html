What the −250 to +10000 band does to the brief

This is the finding that shaped the desk, so it is on the front page rather than buried.

  • Across the whole capture, 16 of the 188 selections we priced carry a chance of 70% or better.
  • 175 of the 188 sit inside the −250 to +10000 band.
  • 3 sit in both. A price of −250 is a 71.4% chance, so "70% or better" and "no shorter than −250" can only ever overlap across a 1.4-point window.

That is why the high-confidence label on this desk asks for evidence as well as probability, and why the picks page shows the band as its own column instead of pretending the two rules always agree.
