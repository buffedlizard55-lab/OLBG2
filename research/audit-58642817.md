## Model and verification limits

`command_model.py`: independent Poisson from the mean of each team's attacking and opponent defensive goal rate; no bookmaker prices, no vigorish, no invented form coefficients, no synthetic quarter-point spread solving. Regulation ties split 50/50 for an illustrative winner probability; handicap and total are regulation-only. These assumptions are not official facts. Fair decimals are reciprocals of unrounded p; American rounding uses p directly.

A high gate requires p >=70%, exact decimal1.40–101.00, **plus calibration**. Calibration is absent, so every experimental estimate is low/medium; no high-confidence write-up is warranted. Higher confidence cannot be created by relabelling a 40% outright or by tuning lines until they enter the odds band. No exhaustive sportsbook settlement or market-availability assertion is made.

Build: `python3 scripts/command_build.py`; structural verifier: `python3 scripts/command_verify.py`. Tests check deterministic output, reciprocal/odds arithmetic, strict band boundaries, confidence caps, live-row suppression, missing-field preservation, official-host source linkage and local paths/fragments. Passing software tests is not fresh external verification.
