Origination rules

  • Win match: Bill James log5 on Laplace-smoothed win percentages (W+1)/(GP+2) from captured W-L(-D) records; EuroLeague uses a logistic on the standings position rendered by the official widget; darts uses a logistic on official draw seedings where the bracket rendered them; the F1 race winner uses a 60/40 blend of season and last-five win shares from the official 2026 results table.
  • Handicap: expected margin from captured PF/PA differentials blended with the win probability (NHL scale 6 goals, URC 14 points, MLB 2.5 runs); priced at the standard ~1.91 two-way. Alternates come from the prior layer where that layer already priced them (labelled inherited).
  • Totals: mean of the two teams' captured per-game combined scoring, rounded to the half. Where no captured over/under splits exist the price is left even and said so.
  • Never inferred: no player names without an official feed, no times/timezones conversions without a label, no lines where form was not verified. Those rows keep the pick blank and appear on Flagged for Review.

Confidence gate

  • High: model win probability ≥ 70% and fair American inside −250…+10000 (70.0–71.4% maps to roughly −250…−233). A 75% favourite at −300 is honestly labelled high (out of band) and kept out of the in-band tables.
  • Medium: 60–70% (or high-probability out-of-band picks' underlying moneyline where the listed selection is an alternative handicap carrying its own price).
  • Low: below 60%.

Capture vs inherited

Fresh capture = fetched from the official league source during this session (NHL score feeds, MLB Stats API, URC match centre, WST match centre, ATP day schedules, EuroLeague official site, PDC official pages, F1 official results, TAURON Liga game centre). Form cross-ref = fixture captured fresh, form label borrowed from the prior /verified/ layer where the fresh feed lacked records (clearly marked on the row). Inherited = entire row carried from data/verified (PR #82) because no fresh capture was possible (NBA endpoints were down; other sports exceeded the session's fetch budget). Inherited rows never claim fresh-capture credit.
