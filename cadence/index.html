How to read the confidence labels. high means the desk's probability for the selection is at least 70% and the official input behind it is a full-season table or ranking, or a 4–9 match current-season sample. medium is 60–70%, or 70%+ on a 1–3 match sample. low is everything else. A price is marked in band when its fair American odds sit between −250 and +10000.
The two rules in the brief meet in a 1.4-point window. A fair −250 is a 71.43% chance, so “above 70%” and “priced −250 to +10000” overlap only between 70.00% and 71.43%. The desk does not widen either rule silently. It publishes every selection's own price and band status, and on the band board it quotes the alternate line at the band ceiling for every fixture whose line ladder allows one. Today 0 of the 22 high-confidence top-five selections are inside the band on their natural line, 8 more clear it on an alternate line, and 14 are genuinely outside it because their probability is above 71.43% — those are listed with their real prices rather than shortened.
