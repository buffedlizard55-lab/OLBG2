Why the predictions column is thin

  • No official strength or form input reached. Darts (PDC order of merit behind a consent wall), Tennis (ATP ranking table did not render in the chunks read), Handball (domestic tables not read; EHF's own calendar does not list OLBG's fixtures), Volleyball (the Polish site redirected to the women's competition), Greyhounds (no official form feed), Gaelic Sports (club fixtures absent from the GAA's national fixture page).
  • Official input existed but was too thin to write to the brief's standard. Ice Hockey and Basketball: the NHL feed gave prices, not club records, and the NBA static endpoint failed; without a record or a recent result there is no form sentence that would be honest.
  • No fixture at all. Golf published nothing; Rugby League published outrights only.

Research aid, not betting advice. Prices here are originated by this project, not quotes from a bookmaker. OLBG supplies the fixture list and its published tip shares; governing bodies supply every other number. Where an official number was not obtained, the value is blank and the row appears on Flagged for review.
