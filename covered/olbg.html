OLBG reverse engineering

How OLBG organises its pages, and which sport IDs and markets this desk could confirm. OLBG was used only as a reference listing. Its prices and tip percentages do not feed any line.

URL schema

  • Sport listing: https://www.olbg.com/betting-tips/{Sport}/{Sport_Group}/All_Events/{sport_id} (example: /betting-tips/Darts/All_Darts/All_Events/15)
  • Sport landing: https://www.olbg.com/betting-tips/{Sport}/{sport_id} (example: /betting-tips/Tennis/3 from search index; /betting-tips/Golf/5)
  • Event page: https://www.olbg.com/betting-tips/{Sport}/{Competition_Group}/{Competition}/{Event_Slug}/{sport_id}?event_id={event_id} (example: /betting-tips/Baseball/MLB/MLB/TB_Rays_@_MIL_Brewers/12?event_id=160789)
  • Outright event page: https://www.olbg.com/betting-tips/Darts/All_Darts/All_Events/PDC_World_Championship_2027/15?event_id=26023

The sport_id is the numeric suffix. Event slugs use '_v_' for head-to-head and '_@_' for away-at-home listings. The 'Today' and 'Tomorrow' labels and clock times on OLBG are shown in the viewer's time zone; the basis was not stated on the pages read.
