Methodology & models

How the 154 originated lines, the confidence labels and the 14 predictions are produced — and where the limits are. This is a research model, not a bookmaker. Rows include source links to the official input page, but a link does not by itself prove the page supported every field; the verification ledger records what was actually checked.

Pipeline

  1. Discover the sport and event on OLBG. OLBG is used only for discovery and market vocabulary.
  2. Verify the fixture on the governing body or league endpoint: ids, teams, local date, start time, venue.
  3. Collect the model inputs from official standings: wins, losses, goals/points for and against, ranking money.
  4. Originate moneyline, handicap and total prices from the sport-specific distribution below.
  5. Grade the selection under the uniform odds-window gate: high when probability ≥70% and the fair American price sits inside −250…+10000, medium ≥60%, low below 60% (ultra-short chalk is capped at medium).
  6. Write a unique ≥30-word prediction for every published high-confidence or band pick, with sourced facts.
  7. Disclose every blank, stale or unsupported value on Flagged for Review.

Confidence and the price band

Confidence

high model probability ≥70% with fair American price inside −250…+10000 (uniform odds-window gate). medium ≥60% or out-of-window chalk. low below 60%. NBA picks are capped at medium because the prior season's records do not reflect current rosters.

Price band (−250 to +10000)

Decimal 1.40 to 101.00. A 70%+ probability is at most 1.43 decimal, so only a 70.0–71.4% selection can satisfy both the confidence rule and the −250 floor. The builder enumerates moneyline, handicap ladders and total ladders, then takes the highest-probability qualifying selection. The 20 original high picks are 1.02–1.39 and are disclosed as outside the band.

Sport models

SportModel and markets
Ice HockeyBivariate Poisson goal grid, 8-game shrinkage to the league mean, +5% home attack, 52% of regulation ties to the home side. Markets: moneyline, ±1.5 puck line, 5.5/6.5 total.
Basketball (EuroLeague / EuroCup)Normal margin model from points for/against, 3-game shrinkage, +3.4 home court, σ=10.8; totals σ=13.5. Markets: moneyline, handicap, total.
Basketball (NBA)Logistic model from 2025-26 win records, +0.28 home logit, margin σ=12. Totals deliberately blank because nba.com standings omit points for/against. Confidence capped at medium.
Rugby UnionNormal points model from league tables, 3-game shrinkage, +4.2 home advantage, σ=12.6; draw band included; totals σ=14.2. Markets: 1X2, draw-no-bet, handicap, total.
HandballPoisson goal grid from EHF group standings, +6% home attack, no draw split. Markets: 1X2, handicap, total.
DartsBinomial leg model from official PDC prize-money rankings; best-of-11 legs. Markets: match winner, leg handicap, total legs.
TennisBest-of-three set model from ATP ranking points. Markets: match winner, set handicap, total sets.
SnookerBest-of-seven frame model from WST two-year ranking money. Markets: match winner, frame handicap, total frames.
BaseballPoisson run model from 162-game runs scored/allowed, 4% home factor. Markets: moneyline, run line, total.
Motor RacingPlackett-Luce-style weights from official FIA points (exponent 1.65) with an 8% residual field share; championship model uses a normal CDF over the points gap.

Evidence rules

  • A fixture is published only with an official URL. If the URL is a competition page rather than a fixture page, the row says so (44 rows in this snapshot).
  • A value that the official page did not return is null, rendered as —, and explained on the flagged page. No value is inferred.
  • No bookmaker price, tipster percentage or secondary outlet enters any equation.
  • Rows dated before 2026-10-22 are labelled past. The snapshot does not pretend to be live.
  • Rows include source links; the audit trail records whether each was re-read on 2026-10-22.

Known limits

  • Sample sizes are small early in the season (3–11 matches per club), so shrinkage is heavy and prices move with one result.
  • No injury, suspension, lineup, weather or travel information is modelled.
  • No home-advantage term is applied to neutral-site or tournament fixtures beyond the venue flag.
  • Futures (F1 title, 2027 rugby league) rest on roster assumptions that are not verifiable in October 2026.
