Method

How the consolidated desk is built

The five published layers are merged by fixture, not added together. Market prices stay exactly as the originating layer computed them; confidence is regraded under one rule; predictions are written only for selections that clear both the probability and price constraints.

The confidence rule

  • High: model probability ≥ 70% and fair decimal price in 1.40–101.00 (American −250 to +10000).
  • Medium: probability ≥ 60%, or ≥70% priced shorter than 1.40. The second case carries the reason “outside the focus band” rather than being silently called high.
  • Low: below 60%.
  • Unlined: no market is published and the reason is on the row.

The band is 1.40–101.00 decimal because −250 American = 1.40 and +10000 = 101.00. A 70% probability is 1.43 decimal; therefore the overlap of “≥70%” and “no shorter than −250” is 70.0–71.4%. That intersection produces the 19 high picks. A 75% favourite (1.33) is outside the band even though it is more likely to win; it is graded medium with that exact reason so the label never overstates the price constraint.

De-duplication and provenance

Rows are clustered by sport, unordered team pair and date (with a one-day tolerance only when the two buckets share no source layer, which is how timezone-shifted duplicates merge without collapsing back-to-back games). 885 layer rows became 382 fixtures. Each row records the layer used, every layer that contained it, the original ids and the capture timestamp. The preferred row is the one with an originated status, an in-band outcome, the most markets and the highest layer priority (verified → hub → board → slate → forward).

What the prices are and are not

Are

  • Fair (no-vig) decimal prices derived from the model probability: decimal = round(1/p, 2).
  • Our own estimates from official governing-body inputs: records, goals, points, rankings, prize money.
  • Consistent across probability, decimal and American odds on every row.

Are not

  • Bookmaker quotes. No sportsbook price is read, scraped or synthesised.
  • A claim that the market agrees. Where our fair price differs from a book, the model is the source of the number.
  • Guarantees. Confidence is a model label, not a strike rate.

Excluded categories

Enforced in the consolidation script and asserted by tests: Soccer / Association Football, Football, Boxing, UFC, MMA, American Football, Hurling, Horse Racing, Cycling, Specials.
