Methodology

Pipeline

  1. First-hand capture — fixtures and team inputs are read only from official governing-body pages/feeds on 2026-10-22 and frozen in data/pulse/inputs.json with the URL of each row.
  2. Origination — scripts/pulse_model.py turns those inputs into probabilities. No bookmaker price is read, copied or implied. The NHL's league-published odds-partner prices are shown on a row as market context only and are never an input.
  3. Grading — a selection is HIGH only with model probability ≥70% and fair American odds between −250 and +10000 (decimal 1.40–101.00). 57.5–70% (or ≥70% but heavier than −250) is MEDIUM; below is LOW. Nothing is nudged across a boundary.
  4. Verification — scripts/pulse_verify.py re-reads every generated page: every table row must carry an official HTTPS source link; blanks must be labelled; predictions must be ≥30 words with per-claim links.

What we deliberately do not do

  • No value is inferred or back-filled — blanks stay blank and are flagged.
  • No fixture from an excluded sport is collected or originated.
  • No tip percentage from OLBG (a community measure) is used as a probability, and no bookmaker price is treated as truth; market prices appear only as labelled context.
  • Write-ups are required to contain ≥30 words, a named team and player, current form, recent verified results and an explicit selection.
