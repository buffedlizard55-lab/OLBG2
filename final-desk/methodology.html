Methodology

Line Origination

Lines are originated from official governing-body statistics using Poisson models (NHL) or baseline home-advantage models (NBA, MLB). No bookmaker prices are read or used.

Confidence Grading

High: model probability ≥ 70% AND fair American odds between -250 and +10000 (decimal 1.40–101.00).

Medium: model probability ≥ 55%.

Low: all other picks.

Predictions

Written predictions are generated for high-confidence picks only. Each prediction is ≥30 words, names the team/player, includes current form and recent history, and states the explicit betting selection.

Sources

Every row carries an official source URL. Values that cannot be confirmed from an official source are left blank and flagged.

Research, not betting advice. 18+. Data captured 2026-10-22 from official sources.
