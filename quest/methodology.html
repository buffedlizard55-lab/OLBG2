Methodology — how every number on this board was produced

1. Scope

Exactly the 15 mandated sports, under OLBG's own taxonomy (OLBG map). Gaelic Football and Hurling sit together under Gaelic Sports. Cut-off 2026-10-23T21:30:00Z: only matches starting after that instant (or dated after it) are listed.

2. Fixtures and inputs (nothing fetched live by the site)

  • Cadence layer (93 fixtures): first-hand official capture 2026-10-23T20:05Z held in data/cadence/ — NHL and NBA feeds, MLB stats API, EuroLeague, Cricket Australia, WST, PDC, ATP, PGA TOUR/DP World Tour, GBGB, LNH, LegaVolley/VBL, Premiership Rugby/URC, NRL (Rugby League World Cup), Formula 1 and GAA county sources. Its prices, picks and prose are carried over unchanged; each price still carries its own basis string naming its inputs.
  • Brief NHL layer (12 fixtures, Sat 24 Oct): fixtures, venues and stored team inputs from data/brief/ (capture 16:30Z, official NHL schedule + stats API). Prices were re-originated for this board by the engine in §4 so that one documented method covers the whole Saturday slate; the resulting money-line probabilities agree with the brief desk's own within four points on all twelve games.
  • NHL 25–26 Oct (8 fixtures): read first-hand from NHL.com tonight; priced only where both clubs have stored inputs (4 games), otherwise listed blank with the reason (flag Q-BLANK).

3. Start times

Five provenance classes, printed on each row: published UTC/offset (52), venue-local converted with DST-correct rules (18), session-window starts for darts (8), viewer-zone clocks converted as America/New_York on published triangulation (9, flag Q-TZ1), and date-only with start_utc left blank (26, flag Q-TZ2). Europe's 25 October clock change is applied by the timezone database, not by hand.

4. The added NHL engine (quest layer)

Expected goals are the simple average of a club's scoring rate and its opponent's conceding rate from the stored inputs, with a 4% lift for the home club (none at the neutral-site game). A Poisson score matrix settles money line, puck line ∓1.5 and totals 5.5/6.5; a regulation tie is awarded in proportion to expected goals, counts as a one-goal margin and adds one goal to the total. League mean across the 27 stored club lines: 3.1552 goals. Where the favourite prices shorter than −250, a band-ceiling alternate is quoted from the totals/puck ladders if one still grades high inside the window — the same convention the cadence layer uses.

5. Prices, confidence and the window

  • Fair decimal = 1/probability; fair American follows from it. No margin, no bookmaker quote anywhere.
  • High ≥70%, medium ≥60%, else low; inherited tier caps (small samples) can only lower a label, never raise it. The labels are probability bands, not a backtested strike rate (flag Q-CONF).
  • Window test: −250 ≤ fair American ≤ +10000. A fair −250 is 71.43%, so high ∩ window = 70.00–71.43% exactly.
  • Picks: match-winner favourite per priced match, plus a band alternate where applicable. Top 5 per sport: matches ranked by display-selection probability (in-window preferred), with the best in-window line shown alongside.

6. Written predictions

At least 30 words each, unique, selection stated in plain words (“X to win”, “Over 171.5”), built only from facts stored with the fixture (form, scoring rates, rankings, venues). Internal process words are banned from the prose by an automated check. No piece asserts an injury — no official availability feed beyond the NBA's was readable (flag Q-INJ); NBA rows link the league report instead of paraphrasing it.

7. Verification (what was actually checked)

  • Every fixture, pick, price and fact row carries an https official link; blank values stay blank with a reason.
  • scripts/quest_verify.py recomputes every fair price from its probability, re-derives every window flag and confidence ceiling, checks prediction length/uniqueness/banned words, confirms no start precedes the cut-off, and crawls every generated page for broken local links.
  • Cross-checks tonight: NHL.com's two published moneylines (flag Q-MKT1), NBA calendar counts (Q-COV1), NHL week strip (Q-COV2), quest-vs-brief money-line agreement within 0.04 on twelve games.
