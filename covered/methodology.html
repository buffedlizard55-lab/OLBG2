Methodology

What was done

  1. Reverse-engineered OLBG's sport and event URL pattern and recorded the sport IDs and market names it shows.
  2. Read the official page for each of the 15 sports (fetch tool, 23 Oct 2026). Each value was transcribed with its source URL. A value the page did not show is blank.
  3. Originated win-match lines only where two sides have comparable official records. Other markets were considered and left blank with a reason.
  4. Applied the high-confidence gate literally. Wrote no prediction for a selection that did not pass.
  5. Logged every failed source attempt and every irregularity in the Flagged for Review page.

Limits

  • This is a single-day snapshot. It is not a live feed and it is not a complete inventory of fixtures for the 15 sports.
  • Bookmaker prices were not read. The desk's probabilities are its own and are not odds from any bookmaker.
  • Several sources returned 404 or a cookie wall. Those sports are blank in this snapshot, not empty.
  • Time zones are as displayed by each source. Where the source did not state a time zone, the row is flagged.

Gates

  • High label: probability at least 70% and at least 20 games per side. Otherwise the label is capped at medium.
  • Band: fair American price from -250 to +10000 (inclusive).
  • Wording rules for predictions: at least 30 words, and no use of the words 'model' or 'verified' in the prediction text.
