The band board

The brief asks for high-confidence picks priced between −250 and +10000. A fair −250 is 71.43%, so that band and “above 70%” only overlap between 70.00% and 71.43%. This page sets out all three groups honestly rather than moving a line and calling it something else.

The arithmetic. Fair American odds of −250 imply 250/350 = 71.43%. Fair odds of −263 (Tampa Bay on the money line today) imply 72.4%, which is outside the band. There is no way to have a 75% chance and a −250 price at the same time; the second number is the first number. So for every fixture with a continuous line ladder the desk also computes the alternate line whose probability is closest to, but not above, 71.43% and still at least 70%. That line is a real market — a different handicap or total — and it is shown with its own probability, price and basis.
