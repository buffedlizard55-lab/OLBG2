METHOD v1.0.0

Originated, not copied.

No bookmaker price is read or reproduced. Every price on this site is derived from official league inputs with the parameters below, so any line can be recomputed by hand.

NHL

  1. Rating. For each club, goal difference per game is multiplied by GP ÷ (GP + 12). The shrink pulls small samples toward the league mean, which is zero by construction.
  2. Expected margin. Home rating + 0.25 goals − away rating.
  3. Win probability. P(home) = 1 ÷ (1 + e−margin ÷ 1.2), which includes overtime and shootouts.
  4. Price. No-vig fair American odds from that probability, rounded to the nearest 5. A 55% chance is about −120.
  5. Puck line. P(cover −1.5) = 1 − logistic(1.5 − margin, 1.2).
  6. Total. Each side's goals for per game is blended with the opponent's goals against per game, shrunk toward 6.0 goals over the same 12-game weight. The line is the nearest half-goal; over/under uses a normal curve with σ = 1.7.

NBA

The 2026-27 season is one game old at the snapshot, so every club carries a league-average rating and only home court separates the sides (2.5 points, logistic scale 11.0). That produces a -2.5 home spread and a money line near −125. No total is originated because no verified league scoring baseline is loaded. These are opening-style lines, not informed prices, and every NBA pick is low confidence.

Confidence

  • High requires at least 13 points of model edge, a games-played sample of at least 7 per side, and an evidence pack: the fixture's own official game page must be cited in the prediction.
  • Medium requires at least 6 points of edge. A larger edge without an evidence pack is capped at medium.
  • Low covers everything else, including every baseline NBA line.

Evidence rules

  • A fixture is published only with an official league URL on an approved host. OLBG is a reference, never a fixture source.
  • A high-confidence prediction is at least 30 words, unique, and names the team, a player, current form, recent results and the exact selection.
  • Every factual claim in a prediction has a source link on the same row, and the fact list is printed next to it.
  • Unconfirmed values stay blank. Blank is a finding, not an invitation to infer.

Known limits

  • The NHL sample is 7-11 games per club, so ratings are heavily shrunk toward the mean.
  • The NBA sample is zero games, so every NBA line is an explicit baseline.
  • No line is originated for a market when its inputs are missing; the cell stays blank and is flagged.
  • Prices are fair odds with no margin and are not betting advice.
