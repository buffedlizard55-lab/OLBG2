Picks and originated lines

One featured selection per match, chosen from that match's own book: an in-band high-confidence price where one exists, otherwise the strongest high-confidence price, otherwise the shortest price on the main lines. Every price in the book is published on the match row.

The two rules only meet in a narrow window. A 70% estimate is a fair −233; the band floor is −250, which is 71.43%. So an in-band high-confidence price has to land between 70.00% and 71.43%, a band 1.43 points wide. That is why 8 of 87 high-confidence selections are inside the window. The alternative — stretching the estimate to fit the window — is not done here.
