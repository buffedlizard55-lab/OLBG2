How the numbers are made

NHL ratings are goals for and against per game, shrunk toward the league mean by 12 games, plus a 0.25-goal home edge, converted with a logistic curve. NBA ratings are league-average because the season is one game old. Prices are no-vig fair odds.

What is deliberately blank

No total is published for the NBA. No venue is filled in unless the fetched page named it. No pick is labelled high without a cited gamecenter pack. Blank means unverified here.
