What is deliberately not published

  • No bookmaker price appears anywhere on this desk. OLBG's tip percentages are named in the OLBG analysis as a description of the source site, never as an input.
  • No injury, suspension or team-news claim appears in any prediction, because no official injury feed could be read. That absence is deliberate.
  • No ranking figure is invented for a player outside a published table. Where a bound is used (snooker, item BR-08) the row says so in the text.
  • No fixture is published from a social post, an aggregator or a secondary summary.
