Methodology

Capture. Official governing-body endpoints only, read through the agent web-fetch tool; each response is stored under data/relay/capture/ (class A) or carried over verbatim from data/current/capture/ (class B). No bookmaker, aggregator or press price is read. When the proxy failed, the row says so rather than substituting a source.

Origination. Attack/defence ratings with games-played shrinkage feed either an independent-Poisson score grid (ice hockey, handball, baseball) or a normal margin/total model (basketball, rugby union); F1 uses a share-of-championship-points outright. From each distribution the desk originates a match-winner market, a handicap grid and a match-total grid, and prices every side at fair no-vig decimal and American from the rounded model probability.

Confidence gate. A selection is high confidence only when p ≥ 0.7000 and its fair American price lies inside −250…+10000. The search covers every originated market and both sides; the published pick prefers the moneyline, then a favourite-side handicap, then totals, then an underdog-receiving handicap. p ≥ 0.70 shorter than −250 is capped at medium; 0.60–0.70 is medium; below is low.

Predictions. Hand-authored against data/relay/band.json, minimum 30 words, quoting only captured official numbers (standings, form strings, records, team leaders), with the exact selection and fair price stated. Tests enforce length, uniqueness and the fact links.

Verification. tests/test_relay_103d996c.py recomputes selected fixtures from the stored captures through an independent code path, checks every rendered link and anchor, asserts every fixture row carries an official URL, and re-runs the band gate over every published price.
