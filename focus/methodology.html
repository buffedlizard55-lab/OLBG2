Methodology

Discovery & confirmation

OLBG's sport hubs (e.g. olbg.com/betting-tips/Ice_Hockey/13, re-read 2026-10-23) define the sport taxonomy and market labels (Money Line, Puck Line, Game Totals). OLBG publishes tipster selections, not prices, so no price here comes from OLBG. Each fixture is then checked on the governing body named in its source link.

Line origination

Poisson attack/defence for ice hockey, baseball and handball (rates shrunk toward the league average, home edge split ±half); normal margin/total for rugby union and basketball net-proxy winner-only; logistic strength-difference for volleyball and rugby league. Fair decimal = 1/p rounded to two places; American is derived from that rounded decimal. Handicap and total lines are half-point lines nearest the expected margin/total. Markets investigated per fixture: match winner, handicap and match total (outright only for Motor Racing).

Confidence

High: p ≥ 0.7000 and fair American between −250 and +10000 inclusive (decimal 1.40–101.00). Medium: best winner price ≥ 60% without an in-band high. Low: otherwise. Blank: no official numeric input captured — price left empty and flagged.

Limits

Strength inputs for non-NHL/MLB rows come from the 2026-10-22 official capture referenced in each row's basis; NHL fixtures/records and the MLB World Series schedule were re-read on 2026-10-23. Injuries are included only where a governing-body page stated them; none were stated for these rows, so none are claimed. Five predictions per sport was the aim; the strict band (only 70.0–71.4% qualifies on a straight price) means most sports fall short — every shortfall is flagged.

Research only, not betting advice. Every row links its official source. Unverified values are blank and listed on Flagged for Review.
