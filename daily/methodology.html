Methodology

One model, declared parameters, no bookmaker input. Everything below is reproducible by running python3 scripts/daily_data.py and reading the JSON it writes.

1. Scope

The 17 eligible OLBG sports exclude soccer, boxing, UFC/MMA, American football, hurling, horse racing, cycling and specials. This pass priced 7 of them. The remaining 10 are named on Flagged for Review with the reason no line was originated.

2. Inputs

Only figures read on a governing-body page are used: NHL standings (record, goals for, goals against, point streak), MLB season records, the URC table (played, won, drawn, lost, points for, points against), EuroLeague standings (games, record, points for and against), the NRL World Cup pool positions, and the AFLW and ICC tables carried over from the 22 October capture. A team with no verified input gets no line, and its fixture is not published on this desk.

3. Model

Ice hockey

Independent Poisson goals for each side, with expected goals blending each attack with the opposing defence: λhome = ((GFhome/GP) + (GAaway/GP)) / 2 × 1.03 and the mirror image for the visitors at 0.97. Moneyline includes overtime and a shootout (0.5 split on ties), the puck line is P(margin ≥ 2), and the totals are 5.5 and 6.5.

Baseball

Log5 on the two season win percentages with a 0.02 home-field adjustment. Moneyline only: the World Series games carry no verified run-scoring input here, so no run line or total is offered.

Rugby union

Expected points from the URC table's points-for and points-against per game, multiplied by 1.04 at home and 0.96 away. The margin uses a normal distribution with σ = 13.0, totals with σ = 15.0, at the handicap lines [3.5, 7.5] and total lines [43.5, 47.5].

Basketball

The same expected-points construction from EuroLeague points for and against, with a 1.6-point home edge, σ = 11.0 for the margin and 14.0 for totals, at handicaps [2.5, 5.5] and totals [163.5, 170.5].

Aussie rules

Ladder percentage, with 4.0 percentage points added to the home side; the two adjusted percentages are normalised into a win probability. No margin or total market is offered, because percentage alone does not give an absolute scoring level.

Rugby league and cricket

A logistic on the published rating difference (scale 38.0 for rugby league, 11.0 for cricket) with a small home bonus. Moneyline only.

4. Prices, confidence and the band

Prices are fair and margin-free: decimal = 1 / probability, American as published. Confidence is a threshold on the model probability — high at 70% or more, medium at 60%, low below. The focus band is -250 to +10000. Because a 70% chance already prices at -233, the overlap between "high confidence" and "inside the band" is only 70.0% to 71.4%; this desk reports both facts separately instead of moving a line to manufacture compliance.

5. Selection rule

Top five per sport, ranked by: selections that clear 70% and sit in the band first; then by model probability. Where a sport has fewer than five fixtures the list takes additional markets on the same fixture and says so.

6. Limits

This is a dated snapshot, not a live feed. It ignores team news, injuries, rotations, weather, travel and rest, and it does not compare its prices with any bookmaker. Two-way markets only. The model has no measured out-of-sample performance, so no strike rate is claimed.
