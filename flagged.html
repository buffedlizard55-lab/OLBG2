What this desk is. A dated, source-linked research record of sport listed on OLBG, of our own originated prices, and of confidence-graded selections. It is not a live odds feed and it does not place bets.

Every price, probability and confidence label is our own estimate, derived from the official statistics named on the row. Values that could not be confirmed are left blank and listed on Flagged for review. Capture times and source files are printed on every table.
