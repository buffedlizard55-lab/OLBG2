How a row gets on this board, how a price is built, and what the labels mean. Nothing here is a bookmaker quote.

1. Event discovery

Each of the fifteen requested OLBG sport hubs was read once on 23 October 2026. Every rendered event became a row: sport, event name, OLBG's displayed start, the most-tipped market, the most-tipped selection, the tip split, and the OLBG event id. Golf returned “No tips found.” The hubs paginate, so the list is what OLBG showed above the fold (F-09).

2. Fixture confirmation

A fixture is promoted to the verified table only when its clubs, date and venue were read on a league page. Ten qualified: six NHL games, three URC Round 4 fixtures and World Series Game 1. OLBG's displayed time is never used as UTC; the leagues' own timestamps are.

3. Prices

Official basis. NHL goals are modelled as independent Poisson counts. Each club's goals for and goals against per game are shrunk toward the league mean with an eight-game prior, the home side gets a +0.10 goal edge, and the away side −0.10. The grid runs to twelve goals. A regulation tie is split 50/50 between the two clubs to represent overtime and the shootout. Money line, puck line ±1.5 and totals 5.5 and 6.5 are read off the same grid.

Consensus basis. Where no official numeric input exists, the price comes from OLBG's published tip split: the selection's share is shrunk toward 0.50 with a twelve-tip prior. A 1-in-1 tip split therefore does not become a 98% probability. This is a crowd signal; it is labelled consensus on every row and is never described as a market price.

Fair odds are 1/p rounded to two decimals and converted to American. The brief's focus band is −250 to +10000, i.e. decimal 1.40 to 101.00 inclusive.

4. Confidence

High: probability of 70% or better. Medium: 65% to 69.9%. Low: below 65%. The band filter is a separate column, because a fair price of −345 implies 77.5% and therefore cannot sit inside a band whose short end is −250. Every such case is shown rather than dropped.

5. Predictions

Written only for high-confidence selections that rest on a governing-body numeric input, and only from figures in the stored capture. Each is at least 30 words, opens differently, and names the competition, both clubs, their records, goals for and against, and the leading scorers where the feed supplied them. No injury sentence appears because no official injury report was captured (F-11).

6. Blanks

A value that could not be read is left blank. It is never inferred, back-filled from a similar club, or copied from a tipster comment. The review page lists each blank and the route that failed.
