F-01 · environment · collection

Sandbox network egress is restricted; OLBG and official sites are unreachable from bash

curl to olbg.com fails with SSL_ERROR_SYSCALL in the sandbox. Only github.com, pypi.org and registry.npmjs.org are reachable from bash. All collection and verification was performed through the harness fetch_page / web_search tools, which do reach olbg.com and official governing-body sites.

Action: None required. Recorded so a reviewer knows why no raw HTTP logs or cached HTML exist in the repo.

F-02 · data-availability · odds

OLBG renders bookmaker prices client-side, so no decimal prices were captured

Live event pages serve the market and selection labels but the price placeholder 'WIN @' is empty; prices are injected by JavaScript. Some older search-index copies of OLBG event pages do contain prices (e.g. Ottawa Senators v Washington Capitals: Money Line 1.61 / 2.60, Game Totals 1.74 / 2.10), but those are historical snapshots of past fixtures, not current prices.

Consequence: We could not de-vig bookmaker prices. Lines are originated from governing-body ratings and published competition base rates instead, which is a cleaner basis for 'our own' line anyway.

Action: If a price feed is wanted, an OLBG API key or a headless-browser collector is required.

F-03 · scope-decision · exclusions

Greyhounds and Gaelic Football were excluded although the brief does not name them

The brief excludes soccer, boxing, ufc, mma, football, american football, hurling, horse racing, cycling and specials. OLBG carries Greyhounds (sport id 28) and Gaelic Football (sport id 25). Greyhounds is a racing family sport and Gaelic Football is a football code, so both were excluded. Neither fits the 'match winner / handicap / match total' model the brief asks for.

Action: Confirm or override. Both are a one-line change in scripts/load_inventory.py.

F-04 · scope-decision · exclusions

Motor Racing is included

The brief excludes cycling but not motorsport, so Formula 1 events are in scope. Motor Racing has no head-to-head match market: OLBG lists Win Race, Fastest Qualifier, Podium Finish, Top 6 Finish, Points Finish, Fastest Lap and Win Tournament. 'Match winner' is mapped to race winner.

Action: Confirm Motor Racing should stay in scope.

F-05 · scope-decision · exclusions

OLBG has no UFC, MMA, Hurling or Specials sport node

https://www.olbg.com/sitemap-betting-tips.xml lists 22 sports: Football, Horse Racing, Tennis, Basketball, Golf, Cricket, Snooker, Rugby Union, Rugby League, American Football, Baseball, Ice Hockey, Motor Racing, Darts, Boxing, Cycling, Handball, Volleyball, Greyhounds, Gaelic Football, Aussie Rules and eSports. There is no separate UFC/MMA, Hurling or Specials node, so four of the brief's exclusions are vacuous against OLBG's own taxonomy.

Action: None. Recorded so the exclusion list can be audited.

F-06 · irregularity · times

OLBG displays event times in US Eastern, not UK time

OLBG lists Afghanistan v Bangladesh as 'Today 07:30'. The official fixture time is 11:30 GMT on 21 October 2026 (ESPNcricinfo), which is 07:30 EDT. Tennis events listed at 01:30-08:00 correspond to the live WTA Japan Open (Osaka) and Ningbo Open afternoon sessions. All listed_time values in data/events/*.json are reproduced exactly as OLBG displays them and are US Eastern.

Action: Site tables label the column 'Listed time (OLBG, US Eastern)' to prevent misreading.

F-07 · source-conflict · cricket

Venue conflict for Afghanistan v Bangladesh, 3rd ODI

Wikipedia (2026 Afghanistan Tri-Nation Series) gives Sharjah Cricket Stadium, Sharjah, with umpires Ahmad Shah Durrani and Allahudien Paleker. ESPNcricinfo's series and fixture pages give Abu Dhabi. Both are dated 19-21 October 2026 and describe the same 11:30 GMT start.

Action: Unresolved. Venue is published as the conflict, not as a single value.

F-08 · source-conflict · cricket

Series naming conflict for the Afghanistan / Bangladesh / Zimbabwe ODIs

ESPNcricinfo indexes the ODIs under 'Afghanistan v Bangladesh 2026-27' with a separate 'UAE ODI Tri-Series 2026-27' node; Wikipedia calls the same fixtures the '2026 Afghanistan Tri-Nation Series'. OLBG lists the fixture generically as 'Afghanistan vs Bangladesh One Day Match'.

Action: None blocking. Competition label on the site cites all three.

F-09 · source-conflict · tennis

ATP official ranking points disagree with third-party mirrors

atptour.com/en/rankings/singles (crawled 7 Oct 2026) gives Sinner 10,950, Zverev 10,070, Alcaraz 5,610. livetennis.io, describing the official ranking published 5 October 2026, gives Sinner 11,000, Zverev 9,720, Alcaraz 5,260. Wikipedia gives a third variant (Alcaraz 5,560, Djokovic 8th on 3,480).

Consequence: No tennis line was originated. Only atptour.com's own top-18 values are treated as official, and none of the 14 OLBG tennis fixtures has both players inside that verified range.

Action: Re-run with the official ATP/WTA ranking tables for the full 1-200 range to unlock tennis lines.

F-10 · source-conflict · cricket

ICC ODI points differ between mirrors for the same rating

ESPNcricinfo's ICC rankings table (updated 19 Oct 2026) gives Afghanistan 29 matches / 2,680 points; Wikipedia (source: ICC, 18 Oct 2026) gives 28 matches / 2,565 points. Both agree on a rating of 92. Pakistan is 33 matches / 3,360 points / rating 102 on one and 32 / 3,215 / 100 on another.

Consequence: Only the RATING column is used by the model, so the points discrepancy does not move any line. Recorded because a reviewer comparing points across sources will see the difference.

Action: Confirm against https://www.icc-cricket.com/rankings/team-rankings directly.

F-11 · source-conflict · darts

PDC Order of Merit values differ across snapshots

The official https://www.pdc.tv/rankings crawl (Feb 2026) gives Gian van Veen £1,035,750 and Gerwyn Price £782,000; October 2026 reporting gives van Veen £1,045.75k and Price £787k. July 2026 post-World Matchplay figures differ again (Littler £3,143,500).

Consequence: No darts line was originated: for all 14 OLBG darts fixtures at most one of the two players has a verified Order of Merit position, so a rating gap cannot be computed.

Action: Re-run with the full official PDC Order of Merit (top 128) to unlock darts lines.

F-12 · data-availability · snooker

Official World Snooker Tour rankings page could not be read; world number one conflicts across sources

Secondary sources disagree: Wikipedia (5 Sep 2026) has Judd Trump number one on 1,655,550 points; snookerhq (5 Oct 2026) has Trump third on £1,114,050 behind Wu Yize; Wikipedia's Judd Trump article (16 Oct 2026) states a current ranking of 3 as of 4 October 2026.

Consequence: No snooker line was originated for any of the 6 fixtures.

Action: Re-run against https://wst.tv/rankings or worldsnooker.com when reachable.

F-13 · irregularity · motor-racing

OLBG's own F1 calendar contains a Grand Prix / circuit / country mismatch

https://www.olbg.com/betting-tips/Motor_Racing/14 lists 'Round 16, 4 October, Bahrain Grand Prix, Sepang International Circuit, Malaysia'. The Bahrain Grand Prix is held at the Bahrain International Circuit in Sakhir; Sepang is the Malaysian circuit. The row mixes three different events.

Action: Raised against the source site. Our Motor Racing rows use only the USA Grand Prix and the Drivers' Championship markets, not the calendar table.

F-14 · model-divergence · rugby-union

Our originated rugby union lines diverge from LNR's own published probabilities

LNR's official club pages publish fixture probabilities. Toulouse v Perpignan: LNR 83.06 / 15.32 / 1.61 versus our 95.10 / 2.60 / 2.30 (12.04 pp divergence, inside the 15 pp cap, pick direction agrees). La Rochelle v Bordeaux: LNR 46.24 / 53.18 / 0.58 versus our 24.73 / 72.99 / 2.30 (19.81 pp divergence, outside the cap).

Consequence: La Rochelle v Bordeaux confidence is capped at LOW despite a 0.73 model probability. Our model weights points difference heavily (Bordeaux +64 versus La Rochelle +9) and LNR's figure does not.

Action: Review whether points difference should carry that much weight in the rugby union model.
