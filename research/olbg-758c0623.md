## 2. OLBG reverse engineering

### Coverage and URL structure

The checked OLBG hubs use `/betting-tips/<Sport>/<sport-id>`; direct events use a sport/league/event slug followed by `?event_id=<integer>`. Shortcut `/event/<id>` routes returned Page Error and were not used. Event pages expose a most-popular selection, tipster counts and comments, plus market families such as moneyline, pointspread/puck/run line, totals, set betting and outrights.

The 125-row `data/olbgdesk/olbg_inventory.json` was captured at 16:20 UTC. A scoped copy is stored at `data/olbg/olbg_inventory_2026-10-23.json`. Its array schema is:

`sport, event, displayed time, market, popular selection, win-tip numerator, total-tip denominator, displayed percent, OLBG event id`.

Those percentages describe tipsters, not win probabilities. They are never pricing inputs.

## 4. Pricing methods

### Two-way log5

For win fractions `a` and `b`:

`p(A beats B) = (a − ab) / (a + b − 2ab)`.

- **NBA:** `a` is the away team’s official 2025-26 road win fraction; `b` is the home team’s official 2025-26 home win fraction. Each is a 41-game venue split. No arbitrary home multiplier or injury adjustment is used.
- **NHL:** exact points percentage is `points / (2 × games)`, then log5. Rounded API percentage strings are not used. No goalie, home or injury adjustment is added.
- **MLB:** official 162-game overall win fractions. No starter, park, home/away or bullpen adjustment.
- **EuroLeague:** official current-season wins/games after Round 7. High-confidence eligibility is off because only 6–7 games and no detailed availability review were captured.

### Ranking-value share

For two officially captured rating values `r1` and `r2`:

`p(player 1) = r1 / (r1 + r2)` and `p(player 2) = 1 − p(player 1)`.

This is used for ATP singles ranking points, PDC world-ranking prize money, and same-format ICC men’s ODI ratings. Ranking-only rows are capped below high. Missing values stay blank.

### NHL total and puck line

Only for the two OLBG-linked NHL games with observed 6.5 and 1.5 lines:

- Expected away goals = mean of away goals-for/game and home goals-against/game; expected home goals is the symmetric calculation.
- Independent Poisson scoring through regulation; each marginal is truncated at 40 goals and normalized.
- A regulation tie adds exactly one goal for overtime/shootout settlement.
- Total 6.5 uses the resulting final-total distribution. Puck line ±1.5 uses final margins; a tied regulation game cannot produce a −1.5 cover.

These extra markets are deliberately **low** confidence. Their probabilities do not override the separately disclosed moneyline method.

### EuroLeague score context

Expected points are the arithmetic mean of a team’s points-for/game and its opponent’s points-against/game. Expected total and home margin are shown as analysis only, not spread or over/under selections.

### Fair prices and confidence

For selected probability `p`, fair decimal is `1/p`. Fair American is:

- favorite: `−100p/(1−p)`;
- underdog/even: `100(1−p)/p`;

rounded to the nearest whole American unit.

**High** requires all of:

1. strictly greater than 70% estimated chance;
2. fair American from −250 through +10000 inclusive;
3. a detailed official fixture/statistics read and, where a standard official report exists, dated availability review;
4. unique prediction prose of at least 30 words with an explicit selection and no prohibited terminology.

Rows meeting only the arithmetic window remain capped and receive a `CAP-…` review item. Medium starts at 60%; lower priced estimates are low. Blank is not a confidence grade.
