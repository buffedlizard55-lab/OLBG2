Sources & methodology

Partial, dated research — not a complete live slate. Only selected fields actually read from official responses are transcribed. Blank means unverified, not zero. Source links and passing tests do not certify every adjacent claim. Model prices are estimates, never official or bookmaker values.

Evidence boundary

This ledger is a selected-field manual transcription of tool responses, not a raw response archive. Some pages were read only in selected chunks; unread objects are not imported. Local shell network restrictions are distinct from agent fetch-page access. The latter returned official content in this session, but not all fields on every source.

Experimental model

λ home = mean(home goals for per game, away goals against per game); λ away is the reverse. Independent Poisson goals are summed over 0–40 and normalized. Regulation ties are split equally for the winner estimate. No home edge is asserted. Historical aggregate scoring is not a verified forecast. Injuries, starting goalies, travel, lineup changes and calibration are not established. Confidence is capped at medium.

Fair decimal is 1/p; American prices use the unrounded p, not a rounded decimal. The focus gate uses the exact reciprocal, preventing rounding at −250 from promoting a disallowed selection. Raw probabilities, not bookmaker-derived prices, drive the estimates. No unsupported props or artificial alternate lines are invented.
