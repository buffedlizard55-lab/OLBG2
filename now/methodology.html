Method

How every number on this desk is made

No bookmaker price is used anywhere. Each probability comes from an official rate on the fixture record, and the same data always produces the same page.

1. The four families

Poisson scoring

Ice hockey and baseball. Expected score = geometric mean of a club's scoring rate and the opponent's conceding rate, so no league-wide constant is invented. Home multipliers 1.06 / 0.98. Ties are split because the money line includes overtime.

Normal margin

Rugby union, rugby league, Gaelic games, basketball, tennis. A mean margin with a stated standard deviation: 13 points rugby union, 12 league, 9 Gaelic, 12.5 basketball, 6 games tennis. Draws are removed by renormalising the two win tails.

Race to legs

Darts. A per-leg chance from the evidence ladder, then a negative-binomial race to ten legs, which also gives the distribution of total legs used for the total market.

Evidence ladder

Volleyball, handball, cricket, tennis and the two rugby league / Gaelic cards. Each side is placed on five rungs using only the facts on the record; one rung of separation is worth ten probability points from 50%. Never graded higher than medium.

2. Handicaps carry the confident opinions

−250 is a 71.43% chance, so a side we rate at 85% cannot be backed inside the requested band at all. The desk therefore publishes a handicap ladder for every priced fixture and puts the pick on the line where our number lands nearest 68%. That is a real, actionable way to hold an opinion about a heavy favourite, and it keeps every published pick inside the band.

4. The 0-10 score

Every pick carries one number so that picks from different sports can be compared without reading the price. It is our chance of the selection winning, read on a 0-10 scale where 5.0 is a coin flip and 10.0 would be a certainty, shaded by how strong the basis is: a price built from a goals or points rate counts at face value, a price built on the evidence ladder counts at 0.8 of its face value. The formula is score = 10 × chance × basis shade, so two picks with the same chance and the same basis always carry the same score, and no pick can score above 7.5 while the band stops at 71.4%.

5. What is never done

  • No bookmaker or exchange price is read, and no OLBG tip share is turned into a price.
  • No value is inferred. If a field is not on an official page it stays blank and is published on Flagged for review.
  • No row is promoted to high confidence to fill a five-a-day target.
  • Displayed times are not converted from one timezone to another.
