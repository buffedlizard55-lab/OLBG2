Methodology

Scope

The brief excludes soccer/football, boxing, UFC/MMA, American football, hurling, horse racing, cycling and specials. The remaining 17 OLBG-taxonomy sports are in scope: Basketball, Ice Hockey, Tennis, Darts, Snooker, Handball, Volleyball, Baseball, Motor Racing, Golf, Cricket, Rugby League, Gaelic Football, Greyhounds, Aussie Rules, eSports and Rugby Union.

Line origination

For each fixture, a model probability is estimated from official governing-body statistics (standings, recent results, rankings, head-to-head form) captured first-hand on 2026-10-22. That probability is converted to a no-vig fair price: fair_decimal = 1 / probability, and the American price is derived from the decimal price in the standard way. No bookmaker price is read, scraped, or invented anywhere in this layer.

Confidence rule (uniform across this repository)

High requires model probability ≥ 70% and a fair American price inside −250 to +10000 (decimal 1.40–101.00). Because 1/0.70 = 1.4286, the only probability window that can satisfy both the 70% floor and the −250 ceiling at the same time is 70.0%–71.4%. Anything priced shorter than −250 is capped at medium even if the model probability is higher, because the brief's focus band excludes it. Medium is probability ≥ 60%. Low is below 60%, and unlined rows show no confidence badge at all.

Predictions

Every high-confidence pick gets one unique, non-templated written prediction of at least 30 words. Each prediction names the competing teams or players, a verified current-form statistic, a dated recent result, and the explicit betting selection with its fair price. No individual fact in a prediction is asserted without a matching official source captured the same session.

Verification and blanks

Every published row links to the official governing-body page it was read from. Where an official input could not be confirmed (for example, GBGB's lack of an advance structured trap-draw card for ordinary greyhound meetings, or a fixture's starting pitcher not yet announced), the market is left blank and the reason is published on the Flagged for Review page. Nothing is inferred or filled in to make a row look more complete than the evidence supports.
