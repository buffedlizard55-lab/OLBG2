How to read this

Prices are ours, not the market's

OLBG publishes tip percentages but no prices, and no bookmaker odds were used. Every price above is 1 / (probability × 1.04), i.e. our own fair price plus a 4% theoretical margin. They are not available prices and should not be read as such. The full formulas live in Methodology.

What "confidence" means here

Confidence grades how decisive the model is and how much verified data stands behind it. High requires a probability of at least 65% from at least seven games of current-season data. The NBA's season is one game old, so NBA picks are capped at medium; the URC has played three rounds, so it is also capped at medium.
