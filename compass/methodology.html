Method

Same house methodology as the rest of the repository (see METHODOLOGY.md at the repo root), applied to the 22:45Z capture.

Pricing families

  • Poisson (NHL) — expected goals = geometric mean of each team's goals-for rate and the opponent's goals-against rate, against the league average (30 captured teams, flag cmp-15), with home multipliers 1.06/0.98; regulation ties split evenly because the money line settles with overtime. Puck line −1.5/+1.5 and a half-goal game total are priced from the same matrix.
  • Poisson runs (MLB) — expected runs from each club's 2026 run rate vs the opponent's conceded rate (baseline 4.5 runs/team/game; no official league-average source was captured), scaled for both starters' season ERA/WHIP (official StatsAPI); extra innings settle the money line so ties split evenly. Run line ±1.5 and a half-run total are priced from the same matrix.
  • Normal margin (rugby, Gaelic, cricket, basketball, handball, tennis) — a margin distribution (sigma 12 rugby / 8 Gaelic / 25 cricket runs / 8.0 NBA points / 6.5 handball goals) centred on a home advantage (2.5 / 1.5 / 2.0 / +4 points / 1.5 goals) shifted by edge terms set from the sourced facts on each record (form, series position, venue, squad quality). Handball adds the 3-way draw as the mass inside ±1 goal.
  • Binomial legs (darts) — leg win rate from the sourced round results and match averages; match probability is the binomial tail to 10 legs (best of 19, official format).
  • Class ladder (volleyball) — 62% (70% for top-two vs clearly weaker) for the better established club; no set-level statistics were captured.
  • OLBG community line — where no official fixture-level statistic exists, the price is the tip share shrunk toward 50% (weight 0.85 / 0.70 / 0.50 by sample size, clamped 30–72%, 30–60% for three-or-fewer tips) and flagged as unverified. Ante-post outrights are capped at low.

Confidence

  • High — numeric family, our chance ≥ 62%, price inside −250…+10000. Totals capped at medium. Unverified-event fixtures and the two-game NBA sample are capped at medium (flags cmp-07, cmp-16).
  • Medium — numeric ≥ 55%, or community/ladder ≥ 70%.
  • Low — everything else.
  • Score = 10 × chance × basis shade (1.0 numeric, 0.8 community/ladder), capped at 7.5 in band.

Honesty rules

  • No bookmaker price is read, scraped or compared — the odds shown on nhl.com schedule rows are ignored.
  • Unconfirmable values are left blank and carried to Flagged for review.
  • OLBG display times (US Eastern) are never used as the official time; official kickoffs win.
