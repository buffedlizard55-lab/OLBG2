Pakistan's two-and-zero record against a winless England side makes them the call

medium · Sun 25 Oct 08:00 UTC

Pakistan · Match winner · -212

Back Pakistan to win the fourth ODI at Gaddafi Stadium. Pakistan have beaten Sri Lanka by 37 runs and England by 87 in Rawalpindi, with Arafat Minhas taking five wickets and making a half-century in the second of those. England have lost all three matches, including a 430-run chase conceded to Sri Lanka after Harry Brook's 202, the fastest double century in ODI history, and the venue shifts to Lahore on 25 October.

Six straight wins make the Titans the pick in Paarl

medium · Sun 25 Oct 09:00 UTC

Momentum Multiply Titans · Match winner · -152

Back the Titans to beat Goldrush Boland. The Titans have extended an unbeaten run to six matches, and Chad Laycock's six for 26 against them was not enough to stop a 21-run defeat. Boland beat Kaneez Tuskers by 63 runs after posting 187 with Lehan Botha making 54 and Gavin Kaplan 38, but they bowled Tuskers out for 124 and now meet the pool's form side.

India's two four-wicket chases make them the call in Wellington

medium · Tue 27 Oct 07:00 UTC

India · Match winner · -152

Back India to win the third T20I at Hagley Oval. India have chased successfully twice at Hagley Oval, winning both by four wickets, after New Zealand made 225 for 4 in the opener with Finn Allen hitting 45 from 24 balls.
