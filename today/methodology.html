Method

How a pick gets on this page.

1 · Listing

Each of the 15 sport hubs is read directly. The event, its displayed time, the most-tipped selection, the tip count and the tip share are copied verbatim into the OLBG list. OLBG publishes no odds on hub pages, so nothing here is copied from an OLBG price.

2 · Official confirmation

A fixture is only publishable once a governing body or league feed confirmed the teams and the start time. That is why 10 of the 15 sports show listings but no pick: their official page either would not return data or was not reached in this capture. The evidence is on Flagged for review.

3 · Our line

Strength comes from the official record: points percentage for the NHL, winning percentage for MLB. The home edge is a fixed 4.5 points (NHL) or 4.0 (MLB). Probability = 0.5 + home edge + 0.85 × (home strength − away strength), clamped to 12–88%. The fair American price is the reciprocal of that probability. For the baseball total, expected runs are the average of each side's season runs scored plus the opponent's runs allowed per game, and the over/under is a Poisson tail on that expectation.

4 · Confidence and the band

High is 65% or better, medium 55–65%, low below 55%. The band test checks only our own fair price against −250…+10000. Because −250 is 71.43%, a "70% or better" rule and that band overlap only between 70.00% and 71.43%; the one pick above that overlap is published with its real price and marked outside the band.

5 · The writing

Every preview states the selection first, then form, numbers and price. Every figure in the prose appears in the fact list under that preview with its official link. Injuries are stated only when an official report was read. None was available for this slate, so the injury line says so instead of guessing.

6 · What is deliberately absent

No price is labelled as available at a bookmaker. No fixture is invented to fill a five-per-sport quota. No value is inferred where a source was silent.
