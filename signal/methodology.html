How these prices are made

Three rules sit above everything else on this desk. Official sources only. No value is ever filled in by assumption - if the governing body did not publish it, the cell stays blank and the row gets flagged. And no bookmaker price is ever an input, so every number here is ours.

The -250 to +10000 band

The brief gives two instructions that overlap: treat a high-confidence selection as one implying better than a 70% chance, and focus on prices between -250 and +10000. A fair price of -250 is exactly 71.43%, so the intersection is the narrow window 70.0% to 71.43%. Rather than quietly widen it, the dashboard publishes three tiers: Tier A for selections inside the window, Tier B for selections above 70% that price shorter than -250, and Tier C for the strongest selections that satisfy the price band. Every table carries an "in band" marker so the distinction is never hidden.

Per-sport method

SportOfficial inputHow it becomes a price
Ice HockeyNHL team summary: goals for, goals against, games played Scoring rates shrunk towards the league mean, 5% home scoring edge, independent Poisson scorelines; a regulation tie is split 50/50 for the money line and resolved as a one-goal win for the puck line.
BaseballOfficial 2026 runs scored and allowed, final standings Pythagorean win rates at exponent 1.83, combined with log5 and an 8% home-odds edge; run totals from a Poisson approximation of each club's run environment.
BasketballEuroLeague standings after round 7: points for, against, games Per-game offence and defence averaged across the pairing, three points of home edge, normal margin with sigma 11.5 and totals with sigma 15.
Rugby LeagueOfficial World Cup results, rounds 1 and 2 Points differential per match, capped at 30 and shrunk by games/(games+2) because each nation has played once or twice; four points of home edge for a true home venue, none in Perth; sigma 16.
Rugby UnionPREM fixtures page: last-three form strings 3.5 points per win of form difference plus a flat five-point home edge, sigma 13. Deliberately cautious because the league table would not render.
HandballOfficial first-leg score of the only tie with a result First-leg margin, plus three goals for losing the away disadvantage and three for the home edge in leg two, sigma 7.
SnookerOfficial world ranking prize money Logistic comparison of the natural logarithms of ranking money, k = 1.1. Players outside the captured top 36 are bounded at the 36th-placed figure, so the favourite's number is a floor.
CricketOfficial result of the first match of the series Unused overs converted at 5.3 runs each into a dominance figure, scaled at 0.0025 per run and capped at 0.80.
GolfOfficial 36-hole leaderboard Win weight = exp(-0.55 x strokes behind the lead) across the captured top 25, normalised. Published as conditional on the winner coming from that group.
Motor RacingOfficial drivers' standings after round 17 Win weight = points^1.6 across the eight drivers published in the captured chunk, normalised and labelled conditional.
Tennis, Darts, Greyhounds, Gaelic Sports, Volleyballsee the flagged page No official numeric input was obtainable today, so fixtures are published with blank selections rather than invented prices.

Caps and guard rails

Published probabilities are clamped to the range 5% to 95%: a single fixture always keeps variance that a season-long rate cannot see. Markets are published as fair prices with no overround, so the two sides of every two-way market add to 100%. Confidence is high at 70% or better, medium from 60%, low below that.

What we do not do

We do not use community tip percentages, pundit opinion or bookmaker prices as inputs; we do not reconstruct a value that was truncated in a captured page; and we do not assert an injury or a team-sheet change that was not published by an official source. Where no availability information exists, the write-up says so.

Every value on this desk comes from the official source linked in its row. Where an official source published nothing, the cell is left blank and the row is listed on the Flagged for review page. Prices are our own fair prices with no overround and are published for analysis, not as advice. 18+.
